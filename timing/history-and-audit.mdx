Recorded timing actions include timestamps and device information. Actions
accepted by the server are retained in the session's timing log; queued actions
still depend on the device that recorded them.

Corrections such as manual times and result deletion are recorded as further
actions. This helps reconstruct how a result changed while those records remain
available. It is not a promise of permanent retention or recovery of every entry.

## Deleting a result and resetting timing are different

Deleting a result removes it from the current results. That correction does not
itself erase the earlier action from the timing log. The current operator screen
shows the result and its correction controls, not a full history or a general
**Restore** button.

**Reset timing data** removes timing records for the selected race or session.
It is not an ordinary correction and cannot be reversed with **UNDO**. Export
records you need before resetting, and confirm the selected scope carefully.
Cleared browser storage can also remove actions that have not uploaded.

## Recent corrections

**UNDO** applies to supported recent actions recorded on the current screen,
subject to your timing role. The recent-action list is not retained through a
page reload. For other corrections, use the rider's result menu and check the
updated result against your backup record.

See [Edits & corrections](/timing/screen/corrections).

## Two devices record the same rider

Separate finish recordings can produce a duplicate flag. Compare the candidate
times and identify the intended crossing before correcting the result. A flag
helps identify a possible issue; it does not decide which record is right.

## Offline corrections

When a device reconnects, its queued actions are uploaded using their recorded
timestamps. A queued correction can conflict with a change already made on
another device. Open **Sync status** and review any conflict warning.

→ [Timing without signal](/timing/hardware/offline-and-sync)

## Keep a review record

Export the local CSV or JSON log from each timing device before clearing or
resetting it. Keep finish-judge notes and other backup timing evidence alongside
the published result. Local exports cover the records retained on that device;
they are not a complete record of every device's activity.

If you need help investigating a disputed result, contact support with the event,
race, rider, approximate time, and available exports. Record availability depends
on what was saved and uploaded; do not assume support can recover deleted or
never-uploaded records.

The organizer or sanctioning body decides protests and official results under
the event's rules.
