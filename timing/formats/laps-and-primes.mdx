For criteriums, short track, and any race scored by laps.

## Turning lap mode on

**Timer setup → Races**. Pick the race (or tick several, or **All races**), then
tick **Count laps** and optionally set which lap finishes riders.

Only what you change is written, so setting a lap count across five categories
won't disturb anything else they each have configured.

| Setting | Effect |
|---|---|
| Lap mode on, no target | Laps accumulate. You decide when riders are done |
| Lap mode on, target of 20 | A rider is marked finished on their 20th counted lap recording |

Each race carries its own setting, so a criterium and a road race in the same
event don't have to agree.

## Timing it

Same as any other race: type the bib each time that rider crosses. The screen
counts accepted lap recordings and shows each rider’s lap total and best lap.
Review missed, duplicate, or suppressed crossings against your backup lap count.

The recording point button reads **Lap** rather than **Finish** once lap mode is
on. Check that label before recording a crossing.

### In a shared chute

Lap races work on the [combined finish](/timing/formats/combined-finish) screen
too. The race sheet grows a **Lap** column (`3/5`) and a **Lap times** column
listing every lap, and a crossing recorded for a lap-race rider counts as a lap.

Timing points can list **multiple recorded passes** as well, so if a marshal at
the top of the climb sees the field on all eight laps, you get eight times per
rider, which is where you find the lap somebody lost two minutes.

What a crossing means is read from the **rider's race**, so a criterium counting
laps and a road race taking finishes can share one chute and one operator
without anyone flipping a control between them. Riders are finished on the lap
that hits the target, and the confirmation says so.

## Voiding a lap

When a race official determines a recorded lap should not count, use the lap
correction control without removing the rider. Apply your event’s rules; a
mechanical, crash, or wheel change does not by itself establish a scoring penalty.

From the rider's row menu, choose **Void a lap**, pick the lap, choose a reason,
and add a note if it needs explaining.

What happens:

- The lap comes off their count.
- Their best lap recalculates.
- If that lap was the one that finished them, the finish is undone and they go
  back on course.
- A note is attached to their result so the reason is visible later.

Voiding adds a correction to the timing record. Export records you need to
retain before resetting timing. The control is available to timing roles with
lap-void permission, including **Timer**.

## Primes

A prime is a mid-race prize for whoever leads on a given lap.

From **More actions → Record prime**:

1. Pick the lap number.
2. Search for the winning rider by bib or name.
3. Give it a label, like "Bell lap prime" or "Sponsor prime".

Primes are recorded against the race and show up in results and exports, so
whoever's handing out envelopes at the end has a list.
