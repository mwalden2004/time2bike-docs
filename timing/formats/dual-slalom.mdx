Dual slalom (and 4X) needs both lanes timed independently and accurately. The
design goal here is that your finish-line volunteers have exactly one button —
because they're watching a rider come down a hill at speed, not reading a
screen.

![Dual slalom](/images/timing/dual-salom.png)

## One phone per job

| Phone | Where it stands | What that person does |
|---|---|---|
| **Starter** | Top of the course | Enters both riders, presses **START** at the gate |
| **Finisher — red** | Bottom of the red lane | Presses **FINISH** when the red rider crosses |
| **Finisher — blue** | Bottom of the blue lane | Same, for blue |
| **Marshal** *(optional)* | Anywhere | Corrections, DNFs, disqualifications |

Three phones is the normal setup, four if you want a marshal. Each person picks
their job when they join the session.

## The starter

The starter's screen has two slots — red and blue.

1. Type the red rider's bib.
2. Type the blue rider's bib.
3. Press the big **START** button as the gate drops.

That's what pairs the two riders into a head-to-head match. Nobody has to tell
the system who was racing whom later on.

## The finishers

The entire screen is one **FINISH** button, coloured for that lane, with the
running time on it. Press it when your rider crosses. There's nothing to select
and nothing to type — that was all handled at the start gate.

**Pressed too early?** Tap **CLEAR**. Your lane goes back to live and you press
FINISH again when the rider actually crosses. Do this immediately; it undoes
your most recent press.

## Watching the match

Tap the trophy icon to see the current match with both lanes side by side, each
lane's running time, and the final result once both riders are down.

Times pair themselves into matches automatically.

## Settings that matter for slalom

| Setting | Use |
|---|---|
| **Pausing** | Freeze the clock — a course hold shouldn't count against anyone |
| **Display precision** | Hundredths. Slalom margins are smaller than a whole second |
| **Allow self-remove** | On, so finishers can undo an early press themselves |

Both are set when you [create the session](/timing/setup/create-session).
