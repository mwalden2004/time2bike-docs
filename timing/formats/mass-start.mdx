Standard mode is what you'll use for almost every race.

![Standard mode](/images/timing/xc-finish.png)

## What's on the screen

| Area | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Race tabs**, across the top | Which race your finishes go into. Check this before every race |
| **Start / Pause / Stop** | The race clock |
| **The big number box** | Type a bib, press Enter, that rider is finished |
| **On course** | Riders who've started but not finished, ordered by elapsed time |
| **Registered riders** | Everyone signed up for this race — tap a name to finish them now |
| **Recent finishes** | The last few you've recorded, with times |
| **Sync dot**, top right | Whether your finishes have uploaded |
| **± figure** | How confident this phone is of its clock. See [How times stay accurate](/timing/concepts/time-model) |

## Running a race

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check the race tab">
    The highlighted tab is where finishes land. This is the mistake that causes
    the most cleanup, so check it every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Start when the race begins">
    Didn't get to it in time? Keep recording finishes anyway. They'll say
    "awaiting start" until you press it, then the times work themselves out.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Record finishes">
    Type the bib and press Enter, as fast as riders come in. The clock never
    stops for you.

    Can't read a bib? Tap the rider's name in **Registered riders** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press Stop when the race is over">
    You can still fix everything after stopping.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fixing things

Long-press (or right-click) any rider's row:

- **Set manual time** — type in a time you took by hand, as `mm:ss` or
  `mm:ss.SSS`.
- **DNF / DNS / DSQ** — didn't finish, didn't start, disqualified.
- **Add note** — anything the director should see later.
- **Delete** — removes a wrong entry from the results. Recoverable.

Marking riders out requires the **marshal** level. Recording and correcting
times needs only **timer**.

→ [Edits & corrections](/timing/operating/edits-and-corrections)

## Two people on the same race

Fine, and often a good idea. Put two volunteers on the same race with their own
phones and their finishes combine.

If both record the same rider, that rider is flagged with two candidate times
and you pick which one is right afterwards — nothing is silently thrown away.

## Lap racing

For criteriums, short track, and other lap-based formats, each pass of the line
is recorded the same way. The screen tracks each rider's lap count and best lap.
If a lap needs discarding after a mechanical or a penalty, an admin can void
that single lap without taking the rider out of the race.
