The default screen, and the one you'll use for most racing. A race gets it when
its format is mass start or anything that isn't an interval start, dual slalom,
or relay.

## Running one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Select the race at the top">
    The highlighted tab is where your entries go. Check it every time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press START RACE when the gun goes">
    The label under the button confirms what's about to happen: *all 47 riders
    go on course*.

    Missed the gun? Record finishes anyway and they'll sit as "awaiting start".
    Then use **Set the start time** and type when the race actually went, and
    recorded finishes recalculate from that start. Review the updated results.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type bibs as riders cross">
    Bib, Enter, bib, Enter, as fast as riders come in. The clock never waits
    for you.

    Can't read a number? Tap the rider in **Registered riders** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Press STOP when the race is done">
    Permitted result corrections remain available before finalization.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Speed at the line

Things that matter when riders arrive three abreast:

- **The input keeps focus.** Never click before typing.
- **Match chips.** Partial numbers bring up matching riders, so you can tap one
  instead of finishing the number.
- **The digit pad.** Big touch targets for gloves and rain.
- **UNDO.** Check the recent action shown before pressing it. Available reversals depend
  on the action and your timing role.

<Tip>
  With a big field, put two people on it. Both type into the same race and the
  uploaded entries combine. Separate finishes for the same rider can be
  flagged for duplicate review. Agree who covers each crossing and compare
  records before finalizing.
</Tip>

## Riders who don't finish

**On course** lists riders with a start and no recorded finish. This can mean
a rider is still racing, withdrew, or had a missed finish; it is not a verified
location list. Check with the race crew, recover missed times, and assign DNF or
DNS only when that status matches what happened.

## Pausing

- **Freeze clock** — elapsed time stops for everyone. Use it for a real course
  hold when the event’s rules call for excluding that period.
- **Pause entries** — the clock keeps running, recording is just held.

Which one you get depends on a timer-wide setting under **More actions →
Timer setup → How I am timing**. It's locked once the first entry is recorded,
so decide before you start.
