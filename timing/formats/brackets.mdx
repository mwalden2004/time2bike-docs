For knockout formats — dual slalom, 4X — the timing screen adds a bracket panel
showing the current match and who advances.

![Bracket](/images/timing/bracket.png)

## The shape of the day

<Steps>
  <Step title="Run seeding">
    Set up a **standard** session for the seeding runs, where riders go down one
    at a time against the clock. Finalize and publish those results — that's
    your seeding order.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create a dual slalom session">
    A second session, in dual slalom mode, for the bracket itself.

    → [Create a session](/timing/setup/create-session)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run each match">
    The starter enters both riders and presses START. Each finisher presses
    FINISH for their lane. The bracket panel fills in both times and the winner.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through the rounds">
    Winners advance and the diagram updates. Carry on to the final. The whole
    bracket lives in this one session.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  **Matchups are set by hand.** Time2Bike doesn't currently build the draw
  automatically from your seeding results — the starter enters each round's
  pairings on the on-deck panel. Have your bracket sheet with you.
</Note>

## Best of two

The usual slalom rule: each pair races twice with the lanes swapped, and the
lowest combined time wins.

1. Run the first leg.
2. Swap lanes, run the second.
3. Both leg times are added automatically, and the bracket shows each leg plus
   the combined total.

## Overriding a result

Long-press a match and choose **Mark winner** to set the outcome by hand — for a
crash, a disqualification, or a rider who doesn't take their second run.

The recorded times stay visible; you're overriding who advances, not erasing
what happened.
