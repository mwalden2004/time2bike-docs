Knockout racing — dual slalom, 4X — gets a bracket panel and diagram on top of
the [dual slalom](/timing/formats/dual-slalom) screen.

![Bracket](/images/timing/bracket.png)

## Setting it up

Run your qualifying first, then click **Setup bracket**.

### Bracket format

| Option | What you get |
|---|---|
| **Single + 3rd place** | Straight knockout plus a ride-off for third. The usual choice |
| **Single elimination** | Knockout, first and second only |
| **Single + consolation** | Knockout plus a full consolation bracket placing every first-round loser — 5th to 8th in a field of eight. More runs |
| **Double elimination** | Two losses to go out |

### Match format

| Option | Winner is |
|---|---|
| **Combined time** | Lowest total across both legs. The standard slalom rule |
| **Best of 2** | Whoever wins more legs |
| **Single run** | One leg, straight up |

### Size and seeds

Pick 4, 8, 16, or 32. Seeds default to qualifying order — drag to reorder, or
seed by hand.

If you have fewer riders than slots, the empty ones become **byes** and their
opponents advance automatically when you resolve the bracket.

## Running it

The **bracket diagram** shows every match. Click one to point the operator panel
at it, then stage and run it like any other dual-slalom pair.

Winners cascade into the next round on their own. A match is decided when both
riders have all their legs recorded, or one forfeits.

**Resolve byes** re-walks the bracket and advances anyone with no opponent. Run
it after setup, and after any reseed or substitution.

## Fixing a bracket

Things go wrong at a slalom more than anywhere else. All of this is available:

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Wrong rider in a match" icon="user-pen">
    **Edit match** in the operator panel — substitute either rider by plate or
    name. Substituted riders are badged so you can see it was a manual change.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Riders on the wrong sides" icon="arrow-right-arrow-left">
    **Edit match** → swap A and B.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A leg time is wrong" icon="clock">
    Fix it inline in the match panel, or open **Edit all times** for a table of
    every match and every leg with editable cells. That's the fastest route
    when an import double-fired or a tag was missed.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A rider can't take their run" icon="ban">
    **Forfeit / DNF** at the bottom of the match panel. Their opponent advances.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The seeding was wrong" icon="list-ol">
    **Edit seeds** re-runs seeding for the whole bracket. Only available before
    any match has been decided — after that, substitute riders instead.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The bracket is in a state you don't understand" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Press **Resolve byes** first — it fixes most of it. If it's still wrong, open
    **Edit all times** to see every leg at once and work out what's missing.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## Seeding runs

Run qualifying as a normal timed race first — riders go down one at a time
against the clock. That's the [interval-start](/timing/formats/interval-starts)
screen, and the resulting times become your default seed order.
