This is the checklist version. It assumes your event is published and people are
registered — if you're not there yet, start with the
[Organizer quickstart](/organizers/quickstart).

## The week before

<Steps>
  <Step title="Assign bib numbers">
    **Event → Bibs → Auto-assign**. Pick a starting number and let it fill.

    Bib numbers are the only way Time2Bike identifies a rider on course, so
    nobody can be timed without one.

    → [Bibs](/organizers/events/bibs)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your timing sessions">
    **Event → Live Timing → New session**, one per group of races that share a
    clock and a finish line.

    A criterium where three categories start together is **one** session with
    three races in it. A downhill and a dual slalom on the same day are **two**
    sessions.

    → [Create a session](/timing/setup/create-session)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Issue links for your volunteers">
    Open the session, click **Tokens**, and create one per person or per phone.
    Label them so you know which is which ("Finish line 1", "Backup").

    You see each link and QR code once — screenshot or print them now. Put them
    in an envelope with the volunteer's name on it.

    → [Tokens & QR codes](/timing/setup/tokens-and-qr)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Do a five-minute dry run">
    Open the session on your own phone, press **Start**, type a few bib numbers,
    press **Stop**. Check the times look right. Then delete the test finishes.

    This is the single highest-value thing on this page. It catches a missing
    bib assignment, a wrong race, or a volunteer link that doesn't work — a
    week early instead of at the start line.
  </Step>
</Steps>

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — The **Live Timing** tab on an event, listing two sessions ready to go.

## The night before

- **Charge every phone** that will be timing, and pack a battery pack for each
  one. A phone timing a three-hour race with the screen on will not survive on
  its own battery.
- **Print your start lists** — **Event → Registrations**, filtered per race.
  You want paper if everything else fails.
- **Print the bib list** — **Event → Bibs → Download CSV**. Your finish-line
  volunteer will want to look up an unreadable number.
- **Set the timing phones to never sleep** while plugged in.

## Race morning

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your volunteers onto their sessions">
    Hand out the QR codes. Each volunteer scans, confirms the card that appears,
    and lands on the timing screen. Watch one of them do it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the sync dot on every phone">
    Green is ideal. Red just means no signal — that's fine, they can still time.
    What you want to know is which phones are which before the race starts, not
    during it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Handle late registrations and bib changes">
    Run **Auto-assign** again to top up anyone who registered overnight. It only
    fills empty numbers and won't touch what's already assigned.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm the right race tab is selected">
    On the timing screen, the tab strip across the top decides which race a
    finish is recorded into. Getting this wrong is the most common race-day
    mistake, and the one that takes longest to unpick.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## During the race

| Moment | Do this |
|---|---|
| The gun goes off | Press **Start** on that race |
| A rider crosses | Type their bib, press Enter — or tap their name in the rider list |
| You can't read a bib | Tap the rider's name in **Registered riders** instead |
| A rider quits | Long-press their row → **DNF** |
| Course hold | Press **Pause**, then **Resume** |
| The race ends | Press **Stop** on that race |

<Warning>
  **Don't switch devices mid-race unless you have to.** If a phone still has
  finishes that haven't uploaded, they live on that phone until it gets signal.
  If you must swap, get the old phone back onto Wi-Fi and wait for the dot to
  go green before you put it away.
</Warning>

## Right after each race

Do this while the riders are still on site — it's much easier to resolve a
disputed time when the rider is standing in front of you.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Scan the finish list for flags">
    The screen marks anything it isn't sure about: a rider recorded twice, a
    finish with no matching start, someone who started but never finished.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fix what needs fixing">
    Long-press any row for the correction menu — change the time, mark DNF /
    DNS / DSQ, delete a bad entry, add a note.

    → [Edits & corrections](/timing/operating/edits-and-corrections)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark your non-starters">
    Anyone registered who didn't line up should be DNS, or they'll sit in the
    results as unaccounted for.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Finalize">
    Locks the race so nothing else can change. It won't let you finalize while
    a phone still has finishes waiting to upload — which is exactly the check
    you want at this moment.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Results go live on your event page, land on each rider's profile, and
    finishers get an email.

    → [Finalize & publish](/timing/finishing/finalize-and-publish)
  </Step>
</Steps>

## After the event

- **Post a PDF** for the awards tent — **Download PDF** on the session, or
  **Combined PDF** for the whole weekend on one sheet.
  → [PDF exports](/timing/finishing/pdf-exports)
- **Handle protests.** You can unlock a finalized session, correct it, and
  publish again. Every original entry is still there.
- **Process refunds** for no-shows if your policy allows.
  → [Refunds](/organizers/pricing/refunds)
- **Check your numbers** — **Event → Analytics**.

## If something goes wrong

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="A volunteer's phone says 'session not found'" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Their link expired or was switched off. Issue a new one from **Tokens** and
    have them scan it. Nothing they already recorded is lost.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Nobody pressed Start" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Keep recording finishes. They'll show as "awaiting start". Press **Start**
    and the elapsed times fill themselves in.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Finishes went into the wrong race" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Switch to the correct race tab first so new entries land properly, then
    delete and re-record the ones that went astray.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A timing phone died" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Anything it had already uploaded is safe on the server and visible from any
    other device. Anything it hadn't is on that phone — charge it and let it
    connect. If it won't come back at all, the finish list from your other
    phones plus your paper backup is your recovery path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Everything is on fire" icon="triangle-exclamation">
    Time on paper. You can enter finish times by hand afterwards from the
    correction menu, and results publish exactly the same way.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
