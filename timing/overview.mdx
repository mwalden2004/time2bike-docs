**Live Timing** is how you time a race on Time2Bike. It runs in a normal web
browser on a normal phone — no app to install, no laptop required, no timing
hardware to buy.

![Live Timing](/images/timing/home.png)

## What you actually need

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="A phone" icon="mobile">
    Any phone from the last few years. A tablet or laptop works too.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your riders' bib numbers" icon="hashtag">
    That's the only rider identity Time2Bike uses. Assign them under
    **Event → Bibs**.
  </Card>
</Columns>

That's it. Signal is optional — see below.

## How it works, in one paragraph

You open the timing screen on your phone and press **Start** when the race
begins. As riders cross the line you type their bib number and press Enter, or
tap their name in the rider list. Each entry is stamped with the exact moment
you pressed the button. When the race is over you press **Stop**, fix anything
that looks wrong, then **Finalize** and **Publish** — and the results appear on
your event page.

## The four things that matter on race day

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="It works with no signal" icon="wifi">
    Every finish is saved to your phone the instant you record it, before
    anything is sent anywhere. If you lose signal in the middle of a race,
    nothing stops — keep timing. Your phone catches up on its own when the
    signal comes back.

    A small indicator in the corner tells you where you stand: green (all
    uploaded), yellow (a few waiting), red (offline, still recording).

    → [Offline & sync](/timing/hardware/offline-and-sync)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Several people can time the same race" icon="users">
    Put four volunteers on four phones and they'll all be recording into the
    same race. Their finishes combine correctly, in the right order, even if
    some of them were offline at the time.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Volunteers don't need accounts" icon="qrcode">
    You hand out a QR code or a link. Your volunteer scans it, and their phone
    is a timing device — no password, no account, no access to anything else in
    your organization. You can switch it off the moment the race ends.

    → [Tokens & QR codes](/timing/setup/tokens-and-qr)
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Mistakes are fixable and nothing is lost" icon="clock-rotate-left">
    Wrong bib, missed finish, forgot to press start, two people recorded the
    same rider — all of it is fixable after the fact, and every correction keeps
    a record of what was originally entered. You can't accidentally destroy a
    result.

    → [Edits & corrections](/timing/operating/edits-and-corrections)
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

## What it can time

| Your race | What to use |
|---|---|
| Mass start — road, XC, gravel, cyclocross, criterium | Standard |
| Time trial or individual starts | Standard |
| Downhill or enduro stages | Standard, one session per stage |
| Lap-based racing with a lap count | Standard |
| Relay | Standard |
| Dual slalom or 4X (two lanes, head to head) | Dual slalom |

→ [Picking a mode](/timing/setup/modes)
