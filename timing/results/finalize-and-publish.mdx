Two separate steps, on purpose:

- **Finalize** — closes the session to new timing actions until it is reopened.
- **Publish** — "the world can see them." Puts results on your event page.

Follow your event’s result-review and protest process when deciding when to
finalize and publish. These controls do not certify a result: official approval
rests with the organizer or sanctioning body. See the
[timing and results terms](https://time2.bike/legal/terms#results).

## Before you finalize

<Steps>
  <Step title="Press Stop on each race">
    Stops the clock. Review results and make permitted corrections before finalizing.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Work through anything flagged">
    Review the issues the screen flags, and compare the list with your backup:

    - A rider recorded **twice**. Pick which time is right.
    - A finish **awaiting start**, meaning nobody pressed Start for that race.
    - Riders with a recorded start but no finish. Check for a missed time or
      withdrawal before assigning DNF.

    → [Edits & corrections](/timing/screen/corrections)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Mark your non-starters">
    Anyone registered who didn't line up should be DNS. Otherwise they sit in
    the results unaccounted for, and someone will ask.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check every timing device shows green">
    This device blocks finalization while its own records are waiting to upload.
    Check the other devices too: a disconnected device may hold records this
    screen cannot see.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Do this while riders are still on site. A disputed time is far easier to
  resolve with the rider standing in front of you than by email on Tuesday.
</Tip>

## Finalize

Click **Finalize Session** in the footer to review the results before locking them.
The review lists flagged times, unidentified riders, duplicate bibs within a race,
and riders without a recorded outcome. Select a result to open its correction
controls. You decide whether a missing outcome means DNS, DNF, or a missed time.

Assign or discard unidentified crossings before finalizing. Then choose
**Finalize**. The server rejects new timing actions while the session is
finalized. An offline device can still hold queued actions, which is why you
need to check each device first.

Finalization needs the **admin** timing role or organization access. Publishing
requires organization access; a volunteer timing link cannot publish.

**Made a mistake?** Click **Reopen session** in the footer, fix it, and finalize
again. Reopening returns the session to *stopped* without resetting its timing
data. It does not recover records previously removed by a reset or lost before upload.

## Publish

Click **Publish**. This:

- Puts results on your public event page, at the same **Results** link
  spectators were already watching.
- Adds each result to that rider's profile and race history.

Check the public page after publishing. Publication is separate from notifying
riders; follow your event’s results-announcement process.

**Unpublish** removes this session’s published results from the event’s result
records. Check the public page afterwards; other imported results or live-sharing
settings can still make event results visible. Copies already downloaded or
shared by spectators are not recalled.

## What riders and spectators see

- A table per race: place, bib, name, category, time, status.
- Sortable by category.
- For brackets, the bracket diagram with each match's times.
- Each rider's name links to their profile, if their privacy settings allow it.

## Handling a protest after publishing

1. Click **Reopen session**. If the results are still published this takes them
   off the site first, and the dialog says so before you confirm.
2. Check the public page after reopening, then review the timing devices.
   Reopening puts the session back to *stopped*; no race starts running again on
   its own.
3. Make the correction using the available timing records and your backup
   evidence. The result menu does not provide an expanded audit-history view.
4. Finalize and publish again.
