<Note>
  **The one rule worth remembering:** nothing you can press loses data. Every
  entry, edit, and deletion is recorded, so any mess can be unpicked afterwards.
  When in doubt, keep timing and sort it out later.
</Note>

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — The sync indicator in each of its three states: green, yellow with a count, and red.

## A volunteer's device says "session not found"

Their link expired or was revoked. Issue a new one from **More actions →
Operator tokens** and have them scan it. Everything they already recorded is
safe.

## Nobody pressed Start

Keep recording finishes — they'll show as **awaiting start**. Press **Start**
and every elapsed time fills itself in.

## Finishes went into the wrong race

Switch to the correct race tab first, so nothing else lands wrong. Then delete
the misplaced finishes and re-record them under the right tab.

## Two phones recorded the same rider

That rider's row shows a **duplicate** flag with both times. Open it, pick the
one to keep, delete the other. Neither is thrown away before you choose.

## The queued count isn't going down

- If the dot is **red**, there's no signal. That's not a fault — keep timing.
- If it's **yellow** and stuck, pull down to refresh.
- If the phone is in real trouble, use **Export log** at the bottom of the
  screen and send the file to your race director, who can load it back in.

## The clock indicator shows a big number

The `± milliseconds` figure is how sure that phone is of its own clock.

- It usually settles within a minute on its own.
- Move to better signal if it doesn't.
- Check the phone's clock is set to update automatically — a phone with the
  wrong time set manually is the usual culprit.
- Pull down to refresh.

Outside of two-lane head-to-head racing, a few hundred milliseconds doesn't
change anyone's result.

## A timing phone died

Anything it had uploaded is safe and visible from any other device. Anything it
hadn't is on that phone — charge it and let it connect.

Prevent it: battery pack per phone, and check for a green dot before anyone
packs up.

## The RFID watcher stopped reading

- The tab went to the background and the computer paused it. Bring it back to
  the front.
- Re-pick the file to start it again.
- If you swapped drives, the browser lost track of the file — re-pick it.

## A volunteer's screen keeps sleeping

Set the phone to never sleep while plugged in. The timing screen tries to keep
itself awake, but the phone's own settings win.

## I finalized too early

Unlock the timer, correct it, finalize again, and republish if you'd already
published.

## A rider says their time is wrong

Look at the row. You can see what was originally recorded, what's been changed
since, and who changed it — so you can answer the question rather than guess.

Fix it with **Set manual time** if they're right, then finalize and publish
again.

## Everything is broken and the race is starting

Time on paper. Record finish times by hand, then enter them afterwards with
**Set manual time** on each rider. Results publish exactly the same way.

## Something else

Screenshot it and contact support. The full history is kept on Time2Bike's side,
not just on the device — so nothing is lost even if the one in your hand is
unusable.
