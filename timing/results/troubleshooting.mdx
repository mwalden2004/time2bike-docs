<Note>
  Preserve the timing devices and backup records while investigating a problem.
  Do not reset timing or clear browser data to fix a sync issue. If recording
  fails, switch to your backup timing method and tell the race director.
</Note>

## A volunteer's device says "session not found"

Check that the link belongs to this event and has not expired or been revoked.
An organization operator can issue a replacement from **More actions → Operator
tokens**. Keep the original device and any queued actions available; confirm
that they upload with valid access before putting it away.

## Nobody pressed Start

Keep recording finishes and they will show as **awaiting start**. Use **Set the
start time** under the start buttons and enter the actual gun time from your
backup record. Review the recalculated elapsed times.

Pressing **START RACE** now stamps the current time. If riders already finished,
it does not reconstruct their earlier start.

For a wave nobody released, use the wave board’s clock control. See
[Waves](/timing/screen/waves).

## Finishes went into the wrong race

Select the correct race for new recordings. Preserve the original crossing times
before correcting the misplaced entries. Reassignment, where available, keeps
the crossing time; simply deleting and re-recording stamps a new action time.
Use a verified elapsed time when making a manual correction.

## Two devices recorded the same rider

A separate second finish can produce a **duplicate** flag. Compare the candidate
records and finish-judge evidence, then correct the result. The flag itself does
not identify which recording is right.

## The queued count is not going down

- Tap the sync indicator to open **Sync status** and read the last error.
- Restore the connection or resolve the reported access problem, then use
  **Retry now**.
- Review any conflict warning before discarding a queued correction.
- Use **Export CSV** or **Export JSON** under **Backup** to preserve the records
  retained on that device. Keep the device powered until its queue clears.

A browser’s online/offline status does not establish that the server is reachable.
See [Timing without signal](/timing/hardware/offline-and-sync).

## The clock indicator shows a large uncertainty

The `± milliseconds` value estimates clock-sync uncertainty. It does not measure
finish-judge reaction time or prove a recorded result’s accuracy.

Check the device’s automatic date/time setting, improve the connection, and let
another sync sample complete. Compare affected starts and crossings against
backup timing evidence. Even a small difference can affect a close finish in
any format.

→ [How timing timestamps work](/timing/how-times-stay-accurate)

## A timing device died

Check the uploaded results from another connected device. Recharge the original
device and inspect its retained records and queue. Recovery of unsynced actions
depends on the device and its storage remaining available; use your other timing
records to investigate anything missing.

Prepare charged battery packs and check each device’s queue before packing up.

## The RFID watcher stopped reading

- Bring the timing tab to the foreground; the browser can pause background work.
- Re-pick the file to restart watching.
- If the file or drive changed, check that the selected file is still the one
  receiving reads.
- Compare the importer’s status and counts against the reader’s original log.

## A volunteer's screen keeps sleeping

Set the device to stay awake while plugged in, where supported. The timing screen
requests a wake lock, but browser support and device settings affect whether it
is granted.

## I finalized too early

With the required access, reopen the session, correct it, and finalize again.
Check all devices for queued actions and republish if appropriate. Reopening
does not restore data removed by a reset.

## A rider says their time is wrong

Review the current result, its start, available timing logs, and backup evidence.
The result menu does not show a full audit history. Record the reason for your
decision with the event’s review records, and use **Set manual time** if a
correction is justified. Follow the organizer’s protest process before publishing
revised results.

## Timing is unavailable and the race is starting

Use the event’s backup timing plan and tell the race director. Record starts and
crossings on paper or another tested method. Once the timer is available, enter
verified elapsed times with **Set manual time** and review the results before
publishing.

## Something else

Contact support with the event, race, device, error message, and relevant exports.
Share rider information only where needed to investigate. Support can review
available records, but cannot be assumed to recover data that was never saved,
never uploaded, or removed.
