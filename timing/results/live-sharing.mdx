Results don't have to wait for the finish. Flip one switch and you get a public
link that updates as you record — for the crowd at the finish, parents at home,
and the announcer.

## Turning it on

Press **Share live results** in the footer of the timing screen. It turns green
and a **Spectator link** button appears next to it — that's the URL to share.

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — The footer with "Live results ON" active and the Spectator link button beside it.

Press it again to turn sharing off. Nothing is lost either way; you're only
deciding what the public can see.

## What spectators get

The event's live results page, updating within about a second of you recording:

- Finishers in order, per race, with times.
- Riders still out on course.
- Marked as **unofficial** — they're watching a race in progress, not a result.

They don't need an account, and they can't change anything.

## Where to put the link

- Post it to your event's social page the morning of.
- Text it to your announcer.
- Put a QR code on the finish-line banner.
- Riders can also reach it from the event page at `time2.bike/events/[slug]/live`.

## Live sharing vs publishing

Two different things, and it's worth being clear which you've done:

| | Live sharing | Publishing |
|---|---|---|
| When | While you're timing | After you finalize |
| Marked as | Unofficial, in progress | Official |
| Lands on rider profiles | No | Yes |
| Emails finishers | No | Yes |
| Reversible | Yes, instantly | Yes — unpublish |

Once you publish, the live-sharing switch disappears — published results replace
it.

→ [Finalize & publish](/timing/results/finalize-and-publish)

<Tip>
  Turn live sharing on and leave it on all day. It costs you nothing, cuts down
  the number of people asking you for times at the tent, and gives riders'
  families something to watch.
</Tip>

## If you'd rather not

Some organizers prefer to show nothing until results are official — fewer
questions about a time that later gets corrected. Leave the switch alone and
nothing is public until you publish.
