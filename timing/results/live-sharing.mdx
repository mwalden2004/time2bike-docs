Results don't have to wait for the finish. Flip one switch and you get a public
link that updates as you record, for the crowd at the finish, parents at home,
and the announcer.

## Turning it on

With organization access, press **Share live results** in the timing footer. It turns green
and a **Spectator link** button appears next to it. That's the link to share.

  <img src="/images/timing/live-sharing.png" alt="Live results ON and Spectator link in the timing footer" />
</Frame>

Press it again to turn sharing off. This changes live-results visibility, not
the recorded timing actions. Already downloaded or shared copies remain with
their recipients.

## What spectators get

The event’s live results page receives updates as records reach the server and
the viewer’s connection permits:

- Finishers in order, per race, with times.
- Riders marked on course without a recorded finish.
- Marked as **unofficial** while timing and review are in progress.

Viewing public results does not require an account or grant timing-edit access.

## Choosing how much detail to show

**Timer setup → How I am timing → What the public sees** controls what appears
beyond the finishing time, on both the live page and published results:

| | Default | |
|---|---|---|
| **Split times** | Shown | Each rider's time at every timing point on course |
| **Lap times** | Hidden | Lap-by-lap breakdown and best lap, in races that count laps |

These controls govern the public display. They do not replace checks for
missing or unsynced records on the operator devices.

<Note>
  The live-results page receives only the split or lap detail enabled here. This
  does not recall data previously shared or control copies of exports.
</Note>

Worth turning splits off when a post went unstaffed for part of the day. Half a
field with splits and half without reads as a rider problem to a spectator, when
it's really a marshal who had to leave.

## Where to put the link

- Post it to your event's social page the morning of.
- Text it to your announcer.
- Put a QR code on the finish-line banner.
- Riders can also get there from your event page, under the **Results** link.

## Live sharing vs publishing

Two different things, and it's worth being clear which you've done:

| | Live sharing | Publishing |
|---|---|---|
| When | While you're timing | After you finalize |
| Stage | Provisional, in progress | Published after organizer review |
| Lands on rider profiles | No | Yes |
| Results announcement | Share the spectator link | Follow your event’s announcement process |
| Turn visibility off | Disable sharing; check the public page | Unpublish; check other public result sources |

Once you publish, the live-sharing switch disappears. Published results replace
it.

→ [Finalize & publish](/timing/results/finalize-and-publish)

<Tip>
  If you share live results, tell spectators that times and placings can change
  during review. Check that the page shows the detail you intend to publish.
</Tip>

## If you'd rather not

To keep live timing private during review, leave sharing off. Check the event’s
public Results page for any previously published, imported, or manually entered
results. Publishing a session is separate from official approval under your
event’s rules.
