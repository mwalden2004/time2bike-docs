You've been handed a phone and asked to time a race. This is everything you need
and nothing you don't.

## 1. Get on the timing screen

**If the race director sent you a QR code or a link**, scan or open it on the
device you'll be timing with. You'll see a card confirming what you're joining —
tap **Continue**. That's it. No password, no account.

The link remembers itself on that device, so if the screen closes you can just
open the browser again.

![Join page](/images/timing/join-op.png)

**If you have a Time2Bike login**, open the event and click the **Live Timing**
tab. That tab *is* the timer.

## 2. Check two things before the gun

<Columns cols={2}>
  <Card title="The right race" icon="tag">
    A strip of race tabs runs across the top. **The highlighted one is where your
    entries go.** Check it before every race.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The right recording point" icon="map-pin">
    If there's a row of buttons above the number box — Start line, a split name,
    Finish — tap the one for where you're standing. It stays set.
  </Card>

## 3. Start the clock

Press the big **START RACE** button when the race begins. The small text under it
tells you what that does to the field.

Missed it? Record finishes anyway — they'll say "awaiting start". Then press
**Set the start time** under the button and type when the race actually went;
every time works itself out from there, including finishes you already took.

Some races don't have this button — interval starts release riders one at a time,
and dual slalom starts per pair.

## 4. Record

Whichever is fastest in the moment:

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| **Type the bib** | Into the big box, press **Enter**. Fastest by a mile |
| **Tap a match chip** | Partial numbers surface matching riders — tap instead of finishing |
| **Tap a name** | The **Registered riders** list. For unreadable or muddy bibs |
| **Use the digit pad** | On-screen numbers, for gloves and rain |

![XC finish](/images/timing/xc-finish.png)

**Two-lane racing?** Your screen is one giant **FINISH** button for your lane.
Press it when your rider crosses. That's the whole job.
→ [Dual slalom](/timing/formats/dual-slalom)

## 5. Fix mistakes

**Just made one? Press UNDO.** It's next to the number box and reverses your last
entry. Use it immediately rather than hunting for the row.

For anything older, tap the rider's row:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Set manual time** | Type in a time you took by hand |
| **DNF / DNS / DSQ** | Didn't finish / didn't start / disqualified |
| **Add note** | Anything the director should see later |
| **Delete** | Removes a wrong entry from the results |

![Result actions](/images/timing/result-actions.png)

Nothing here is permanent damage — the original entry is always recoverable, and
a director can undo anything.

## 6. About signal

**You don't need it.** Every entry is saved to your device the moment you record
it. In a canyon with no bars, keep timing exactly as normal.

The dot in the corner tells you where things stand:

| | Meaning | Do you need to do anything? |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | Everything uploaded | No |
| 🟡 | A few entries waiting to upload | No — they'll go when there's signal |
| 🔴 | No signal, recording to this device | No — keep timing |

<Warning>
  If the dot isn't green, your entries haven't reached anyone else yet.
  **Don't put the device away, hand it off, or let it die** until it goes green.
  Walk it somewhere with signal if you have to.
</Warning>

## 7. Someone turns up on the day

**More actions → Add riders.** Bib, name, team, race. Works with no signal.

Collect their waiver and money on paper — the timing screen doesn't do either.
→ [Adding riders](/timing/screen/adding-riders)

## 8. When the race ends

1. Press **STOP** on the race.
2. Look down the finish list. Sort out anything flagged while riders are still
   around.
3. Mark anyone who didn't start as **DNS**.
4. Hand the device back — or if you have the access, **Finalize**, then
   **Publish**.

## Cheat sheet

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Which race am I recording into | Tab strip across the top |
| Where am I standing | Recording-point buttons above the number box |
| Record someone | Big number box → Enter |
| Undo what I just did | **UNDO**, next to the number box |
| Riders still out there | "On course" list |
| Tap someone in instead of typing | "Registered riders" list |
| Fix an older entry | Tap that rider's row |
| Have my entries uploaded? | Coloured dot, top right |
| Add a day-of rider | More actions → Add riders |
| Lock the results | **Finalize Session**, in the footer |
| Put results on the website | **Publish**, after finalize |
| Full built-in reference | **Help**, top right |

## If something looks wrong

Take a screenshot and tell the race director. Nothing you can press loses data —
every entry, edit, and deletion is recorded, so a problem can always be unpicked
afterwards.
