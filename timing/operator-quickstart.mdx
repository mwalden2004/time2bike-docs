Use this field guide alongside the race director's briefing and your backup
timing plan.

## 1. Get on the timing screen

**If the race director sent you a QR code or a link**, scan or open it on the
device you'll be timing with. You'll see a card confirming what you're joining,
then tap **Continue**. That's it. No password, no account.

The browser stores the timing link locally. Keep a copy available in case
browser data is cleared, and check its expiry before the race.

tab. That tab *is* the timer.

## 3. Start the clock

Press the big **START RACE** button when the race begins. The small text under it
tells you what that does to the field.

Missed it? Record finishes anyway and they'll say "awaiting start". Then press
**Set the start time** under the button and type when the race actually went;
recorded finishes are recalculated from that start. Check the updated results.

Some races don't have this button. Interval starts release riders one at a time,
and dual slalom starts per pair.

## 4. Record

Whichever is fastest in the moment:

| Way | How |
|---|---|
| **Type the bib** | Into the big box, press **Enter**. Fastest by a mile |
| **Tap a match chip** | Partial numbers surface matching riders — tap instead of finishing |
| **Tap a name** | The **Registered riders** list. For unreadable or muddy bibs |
| **Use the digit pad** | On-screen numbers, for gloves and rain |

**Two-lane racing?** Your screen is one giant **FINISH** button for your lane.
Press it when your rider crosses. That's the whole job.
→ [Dual slalom](/timing/formats/dual-slalom)

## 5. Fix mistakes

**Just made a mistake? Check UNDO.** It is next to the number box and identifies
the recent action it can reverse. Availability depends on the action and your
role; the recent-action list does not survive a reload.

For anything older, tap the rider's row:

| Option | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Set manual time** | Type in a time you took by hand |
| **DNF / DNS / DSQ** | Didn't finish / didn't start / disqualified |
| **Delete time** | Removes a wrong entry from the results |

Check the corrected result against your backup timing record. Avoid **Reset
timing data** during correction work: it removes records and is not an undo step.

## 6. About signal

A prepared timing screen can record locally during a connection loss while
device storage is working. Check that each action appears and respond to any
storage or recording error. See [Timing without signal](/timing/hardware/offline-and-sync).

The dot in the corner tells you where things stand:

| | Meaning | Do you need to do anything? |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 | No actions queued on this device | Check other devices before finalizing |
| 🟡 | Actions waiting to upload | Tap for progress, errors, and retry |
| 🔴 | Browser reports no connection | Keep powered; check local records |

<Warning>
  Queued actions may exist only on this device until the server accepts them.
  **Don't put the device away, hand it off, or let it die** until it goes green.
  Walk it somewhere with signal if you have to.
</Warning>

## 7. Someone turns up on the day

**More actions → Register a rider** opens registration for authorized
organization operators while connected. If you are offline, use the unidentified
bib workflow and follow the organizer’s registration, waiver, and payment
procedure before allowing an entry.
→ [Adding riders](/timing/screen/adding-riders)

## 8. When the race ends

1. Press **STOP** on the race.
2. Look down the finish list. Sort out anything flagged while riders are still
   around.
3. Mark anyone who didn't start as **DNS**.
4. Hand the device back, or if you have the access, **Finalize**, then
   **Publish**.

## Cheat sheet

| What | Where |
|---|---|
| Which race am I recording into | Tab strip across the top |
| Where am I standing | Recording-point buttons above the number box |
| Record someone | Big number box → Enter |
| Undo what I just did | **UNDO**, next to the number box |
| Riders without a recorded finish | "On course" list; verify with the race crew |
| Tap someone in instead of typing | "Registered riders" list |
| Fix an older entry | Tap that rider's row |
| Have my entries uploaded? | Coloured dot, top right |
| Add a day-of rider | More actions → Register a rider; connection required |
| Lock the results | **Finalize Session**, in the footer |
| Put results on the website | **Publish**, after finalize |
| Full built-in reference | **Help**, top right |

## If something looks wrong

Tell the race director and save the relevant record or screenshot. If uploads
are failing, retain the device and export its local log. Use your backup timing
plan if recording itself is failing; do not reset or clear site data to troubleshoot.
