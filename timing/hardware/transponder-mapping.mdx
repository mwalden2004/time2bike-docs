title: "Transponder mapping"
description: "Connect RFID chip IDs to registered riders."
---

Before any RFID ingestion is useful, each transponder ID has to be mapped to a participant. This happens at **Event → Transponders** (see [the organizer page](/organizers/events/transponders)).

[IMAGE: /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/transponders → transponder assignment list]

## Add one

Enter the **transponder ID** (whatever your reader reports — typically a hex code) and pick the **participant** from the dropdown. Click **Add**.

## Bulk pickup workflow

At chip pickup the morning of the race:

1. Open the transponders page on a tablet.
2. For each rider arriving:
   - Scan a chip into the **transponder ID** field.
   - Pick the rider from the dropdown (filtered by start list).
   - Click **Add**.

The page auto-loads the start list from the active timing session, so dropdowns show name + plate even before bibs are physically attached.

## After the race

If you discover a transponder was unmapped at race time, add the mapping now and re-trigger projection — past `external_rfid` events for that chip are re-resolved to the participant.
