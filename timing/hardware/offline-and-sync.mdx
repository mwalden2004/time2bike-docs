title: "Timing without signal"
description: "What happens when the network drops, and the one thing you shouldn't do."
icon: "wifi"
---

Every finish is saved to your phone the instant you record it, before anything
is sent anywhere. **Signal is a bonus, not a requirement.** If the network drops
mid-race, keep timing exactly as normal.

## The dot in the corner

| | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Green** | Everything on this phone has uploaded | Nothing |
| 🟡 **Yellow** | Some finishes are waiting to upload | Nothing — they'll go when there's signal |
| 🔴 **Red** | No signal, recording to this phone | Nothing — keep timing |

Yellow and red are normal at a bike race. They don't mean anything is wrong.

## The one thing to be careful about

<Warning>
  If the dot isn't green, the finishes on that phone exist **only on that
  phone**. Don't put it away, hand it off, or let it die until it's green.

  If you need to move to a different device mid-race, walk the old phone
  somewhere with signal and wait for green first.
</Warning>

This is the only way to actually lose timing data, and it's avoidable.

## What survives what

| If this happens | What you get |
|---|---|
| You refresh or close the tab | Everything's still there when you reopen it |
| Signal drops for an hour | You keep timing. It all uploads on reconnect |
| Your battery dies mid-race | Anything already uploaded is safe. Anything not, is on that phone — charge it and let it connect |
| You switch to a spare phone | The spare loads everything from the server. Anything the old phone hadn't uploaded still needs the old phone |
| Time2Bike has a hiccup | Nothing is lost. Your phone keeps recording and catches up |

## When it uploads

Automatically — as soon as signal returns, whenever you switch back to the tab,
and periodically while you're idle. You don't have to trigger it.

If you want to nudge it, pull down on the page to refresh.

A phone that's been offline for a long stretch sorts itself out within a few
seconds of reconnecting, and slots its finishes into the right places in the
results rather than tacking them on the end.

## The rescue export

At the bottom of the timing screen there's an **Export log** button. It saves
everything that phone has recorded to a file.

Use it if a phone is in trouble and you want the finishes off it before anything
worse happens — a dying battery, a cracked screen, a phone that won't reconnect.
Send the file to your race director, who can load it back in.

Think of it as the fire extinguisher. You'll probably never need it, and you'll
be glad it's there if you do.

## Practical advice

- **Battery packs for every timing phone.** A phone with the screen on for three
  hours will not make it on its own.
- **Set timing phones to never sleep** while charging.
- **Check for green before you pack up.** Make it the last thing you do at each
  finish line.
