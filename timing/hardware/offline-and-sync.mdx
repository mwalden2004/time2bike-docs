The timer writes recorded actions to browser storage before attempting to upload
them. A device that has loaded the timing screen and session can keep recording
during a connection loss, provided its local storage is working.

Before race day, load the timer on each device, use **More actions → Download for
offline**, and test reopening it without a connection. Keep a backup timing
record. Offline availability depends on the browser retaining the page and data.

## The sync indicator

| Status | Meaning | What to do |
|---|---|---|
| 🟢 **Synced** | No actions are waiting to upload on this device | Check the other timing devices before finalizing |
| 🟡 **Queued** | Actions are waiting to upload | Tap the indicator to check progress or errors |
| 🔴 **Offline** | The browser reports no connection | Keep the device powered and monitor local recording |

An offline indicator does not confirm that every crossing was saved. Check the
recorded entries and respond to storage or recording errors.

## Protect queued records

<Warning>
  Queued actions have not been confirmed by the server and may exist only on
  that device. Keep it powered and retain access to it until the queue clears.
  Do not clear site data, reset timing, or discard queued actions as a way to
  fix a connection problem.
</Warning>

Browser storage can be cleared or evicted, and a full device can reject new
writes. Treat storage warnings promptly. Avoid private browsing for timing, and
export the local log if the device or connection is in trouble.

## What to expect

| Situation | Next step |
|---|---|
| You refresh or close the tab | Reopen the same browser and timer; check that retained records are present |
| Signal drops | Keep recording if local saves are working; monitor the queue when connectivity returns |
| The battery dies | Recharge the device and check its retained queue; compare with your backup record |
| You switch to a spare device | The spare can load server records when connected; recover unsynced actions from the original device |
| The server is unavailable | Local recording can continue; uploads need the service and connection to recover |

## Uploads and retrying

The open timing page retries automatically, including when connectivity returns
or you switch back to the tab. Upload time depends on the connection, queue size,
and any server or access errors. A closed or suspended tab is not a dependable
way to finish uploading.

Tap the sync indicator to see **Sync status**. Review the last error and use
**Retry now** after addressing it. A correction made offline can conflict with
another operator's change; review that warning before discarding either record.

## Export the local log

Open **Sync status** and use **Export CSV** or **Export JSON** under **Backup**.
These download the timing actions retained on that device. Keep the file with
your other race records and contact the race director or support for help
reviewing it. An export does not confirm that those actions reached the server,
and automatic recovery from every exported file is not guaranteed.

## Check-in while offline

The [check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) also uses a cached roster and a
local queue. Load the roster while connected and check its offline/queued banner.
New registrations and changes from other devices require a connection to arrive.
Keep the tablet available until its queued check-ins have uploaded.

## Practical preparation

- Charge each device and bring a battery pack sized for the event.
- Set timing devices to stay awake while charging, where supported.
- Keep the timing tab open and visible during recording and upload.
- Check every device's queue and the resulting finish list before packing up.
