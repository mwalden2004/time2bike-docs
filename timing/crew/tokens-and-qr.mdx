A **timing link** turns any device into a timing device for one event. Your
volunteer scans a QR code and starts timing, with no account, no password, and
no access to anything else you run.

![Token QR](/images/timing/qr-invite.png)

## Create one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open More actions → Operator tokens">
    You need to be an organization member, or hold a timing link with admin
    access.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Issue new token">
    Fill in:

    | Field | What to put |
    |---|---|
    | **Label** | For you, not them — "Finish line phone 1", "Sarah's phone" |
    | **Roles** | What they're allowed to do. **Timer** for most volunteers — see [Who can do what](/timing/crew/roles) |
    | **Expires in** | Hours from now. Set it to just past the end of your event |
    | **Allowed races** | Optional. Restricts them to specific races in the event |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the QR code before you close the dialog">
    You see the link and QR code **once**. Screenshot it, print it, or copy it
    somewhere now.

    If you lose it, no harm done. Revoke it and issue another.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Issue one link **per phone**, not per person. If a volunteer's phone dies and
  they borrow another, you want to be able to switch off the dead one without
  cutting off everyone else.
</Tip>

## Handing them out

Print each QR code with the volunteer's name on it and put them in envelopes.
It's the fastest possible race-morning handover: one piece of paper, one scan,
they're timing.

Or text the link. Either works.

## What your volunteer sees

They open the QR code or link on their phone and get a confirmation card showing
which organization and event they're joining and what they'll be able to do.
They tap **Continue** and they're on the timing screen.

![Join page](/images/timing/join-op.png)

The link saves itself on that phone, so closing the browser doesn't lock them
out. They can pick it up again all day.

## Switching one off

Click **Revoke** next to any link in the token list. That device stops working
immediately.

Anything they already recorded is unaffected. Revoking access never removes
results. If they're mid-race, issue a new link and have them scan it; nothing is
lost in between.

## What a timing link can't do

- Reach any other event or race in your organization.
- See registrations, rider details, or anything financial.
- Reconfigure the timer, or wipe its data.
- Issue further links, unless you gave it the admin role.

This is enforced on Time2Bike's side, not just hidden in the interface. A
volunteer link genuinely cannot reach the rest of your organization.
