A **timing link** grants event timing access on a supported device. Your
volunteer scans a QR code and starts timing, without needing an account or password. The link exposes the event’s timing
roster and results, so share it privately with the intended operator.

## Create one

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open More actions → Operator tokens">
    Use your organization account with the required event access. Volunteer
    timing links, including those with **Admin**, cannot issue more links.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Issue new token">
    Fill in:

    | Field | What to put |
    |---|---|
    | **Label** | For you, not them — "Finish line phone 1", "Backup phone" |
    | **Roles** | What they're allowed to do. **Timer** for most volunteers — see [Who can do what](/timing/crew/roles) |
    | **Expires in** | Hours from now. Allow time after the event for devices to finish syncing |
    | **Allowed races** | Optional. Restricts which races their timing actions can target |
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the QR code before you close the dialog">
    You see the link and QR code **once**. Screenshot it, print it, or copy it
    somewhere now.

    If you lose it, revoke it and issue another. Preserve any queued records
    on a device using the old link and confirm upload with replacement access.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Issue one link **per phone**, not per person. If a volunteer's phone dies and
  they borrow another, you want to be able to switch off the dead one without
  cutting off everyone else.
</Tip>

## Handing them out

Print each QR code with the volunteer's name on it and put them in envelopes.
Have each operator confirm the event and role, then test their assigned
recording controls before the race.

Or text the link. Either works.

## What your volunteer sees

They open the QR code or link on their phone and get a confirmation card showing
which organization and event they're joining and what they'll be able to do.
They tap **Continue** and they're on the timing screen.

The browser stores the link locally while that data is retained. Keep a copy
available, check its expiry, and test offline access before leaving coverage.

## Switching one off

Click **Revoke** next to the link. Subsequent authenticated requests using it
are rejected. Cached information can remain on an open or offline device;
revocation does not erase it remotely.

Revocation does not delete uploaded timing records. It can prevent a device’s
queued actions from uploading, so coordinate replacement access and preserve
its local records when timing is still in progress.

## What a timing link can't do

- Grant general organization membership.
- Publish or unpublish results, or change live sharing.
- Reset timing data.
- Issue or revoke other timing links.

An **Admin** timing link can configure the session and finalize or reopen it.
Other recording and correction permissions depend on the assigned roles. See
[Who can do what](/timing/crew/roles).
