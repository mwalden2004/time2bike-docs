A timing link grants access to one event’s timer. Choose the smallest role that
covers the operator’s job, and test that role’s controls before the race.

| Role | Timing access |
|---|---|
| **View** | View timing data without recording actions |
| **Timer** | Start and stop races, record crossings, and use permitted lap, split, lane, and bracket corrections |
| **Marshal** | Timer actions plus manual times, result-status changes, deletion, and additional bracket/roster controls |
| **Admin** | Additional session controls, including configuration, finalization, and reopening |
| **Refund** | No timing actions currently assigned to this role |

**Timer** access can affect times and bracket outcomes. Do not treat it as
permission only to add a finish or as protection against operator mistakes.
Choose operators who understand the controls they will use.

Roles can be combined. **Marshal** already includes the timer’s recording actions.

## Scope of a timing link

Timing links expose the event’s timing roster and results, including rider names
and bibs. Share them privately, and use the public spectator link for people who
only need public results.

A timing link does not grant general organization membership. Optional **Allowed
races** restrict the races its actions can target. A timing link cannot publish
or unpublish results, change live sharing, issue or revoke other timing links,
or reset timing data; those actions require appropriate organization access.

## Organization members

Organization members use their normal sign-in to open **Live Timing**. During
beta, organization membership gives broad access regardless of the assigned
organization role. Use timing links when you need narrower event timing access,
and review organization membership separately.

## Revoking access

An organization operator can open **More actions → Operator tokens** and click
**Revoke**. Subsequent requests using that link are rejected. An already-open
screen or offline device can still display cached data; revocation does not
remotely erase it.

Uploaded timing actions remain in the session. If a legitimate device has actions
waiting to upload, coordinate replacement access and preserve its local records.
Revoking or expiring a link can prevent its queue from uploading.

Set the expiry to cover the event and the time needed to finish syncing.
