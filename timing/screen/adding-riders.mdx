You can open the registration form without leaving the timing screen. With a
connection, choose **More actions → Register a rider**.

<Frame caption="Demo data.">
  <img src="/images/organizers/manual-entry.png" alt="Manual entry dialog for a demo event" />
</Frame>

## Register a rider with a connection

Use the same form as the event's **Participants** page to find an existing rider
or create an entry. Pick their races, review the day-of price, and handle their
payment and plate assignment.

Finding an existing rider first helps you avoid creating a second profile for
the same person.

Follow [manual entry](/organizers/registrations/manual-entry) for the full
registration steps, including payment and waiver options.

## Record an unknown bib while offline

**Register a rider** needs a connection. If you are offline and a rider crosses
the line without a matching start-list entry, enter their bib in the timing
box. The timer can record them as an unidentified rider and sync that entry
when the connection returns.

Finish their registration and payment on the **Participants** page once you are
online. Identify the rider before publishing the results.

<Note>
  Recording an unknown bib captures a timing result. Complete the event's
  required registration, waiver, and payment steps separately.
</Note>

## Who can open the form

The registration form is available to organization operators with the required
access. Volunteer timing links do not show **Register a rider**.
