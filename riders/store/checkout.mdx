## What you'll enter

- **Email** — for the receipt and pickup info. Pre-filled if you're
  signed in.
- **Name** — name on the order, for pickup.
- **Fulfillment**:
  - **Pickup** — collect at the event the organizer designated.
  - **Ship** — enter a shipping address. Only available when **every**
    item in your cart has shipping enabled by the organizer. Mixed
    carts (a shippable hat with a pickup-only race shirt) force pickup
    for the whole order.
- **Shipping address** — line 1, optional line 2, city, optional
  state/region, postal code, country. Required when you pick **Ship**.
- **Discount code** (optional). If the organizer gave you one, type
  it in and click **Apply**. The cart re-validates the code whenever
  you change quantities. Review the updated total before paying.

You don't need a Time2Bike account to buy merch, but signing in pre-fills
your info and ties the order to your account history.

## Totals

The cart shows:

- **Subtotal**, the sum of all line items.
- **Discount**, subtracted from the subtotal if a valid code is applied.
- **Service fee**, calculated at the percentage shown in your cart after
  any discount.
- **Total** — what your card will be charged.

If a discount brings your total to **$0.00**, you skip Stripe entirely
and land straight on the confirmation page, with no card needed.

## Checking out

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Checkout">
    For paid orders you're redirected to Stripe Checkout. The discount
    appears as its own line on the Stripe page so you can see exactly
    what's coming off.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pay">
    Stripe handles the card and any wallet payments the org has enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on the confirmation page">
    It confirms the order and empties your cart.

    <Frame caption="Store order confirmation. Demo checkout; no payment was made.">
      <img src="/images/riders/merch-finish.png" alt="Store confirmation page with a Thank you message and Continue shopping button" />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Pickup vs shipping

Check the product's pickup or shipping terms before ordering. The organizer
handles fulfillment; an order confirmation does not mean an item has shipped
or is ready for collection. Contact the organizer about delivery, pickup times,
or a pickup order you cannot collect.

## Order history

Keep your confirmation email and payment reference. You can review available
Stripe payment history from [Account → Billing](/riders/account/billing-history).
For order details or a missing receipt, contact the organizer.
