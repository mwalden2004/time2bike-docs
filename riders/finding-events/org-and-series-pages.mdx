## Organization page

Every organization gets a public page, for example `time2.bike/socalmtb`.
It shows:

- The organization's logo, name, and description
- A list of **upcoming events**, each linking to its event page
- A list of **past events** with links to results
- Featured **sponsors**
- A link to the **merch store** if the org has one

![Org Page](/images/riders/org.png)

The organizer chooses that address themselves. If they ever change it, the
old one stops working, so use the most recent link they've shared rather
than an old bookmark.

## Series page

A **series** is a collection of related events scored together, like a
6-round summer race series. Series pages look like
`time2.bike/series/summer-gravel-cup`.

A series page shows:

- The series name and description
- All **rounds** (events) in the series, in order
- Optional **series standings** once at least one round has finished
- **Season plates** — the number policy, and your own number
- Sponsors and the organizing org

![Series Page](/images/riders/series.png)

### Season plates

Where a series keeps the same number on a rider all season, the page says so —
whether you bring the plate you already have to each round, and what a
replacement costs.

Sign in and it also shows **your** number: the plate, who it belongs to (yours
and anyone you manage), how long you've held it, and how many rounds you've used
it at. Only your own — it isn't a public roster of everybody's numbers.

If you don't have one yet, it says that too: you're given one when you enter
your first round.

→ [Series plates](/organizers/series/series-plates)

## Finding an event

Right now Time2Bike doesn't have a global event search. You find events
by following an organization or series link. Most organizers share their
event page directly via email, social media, or QR codes printed at the
finish line of their other events.
