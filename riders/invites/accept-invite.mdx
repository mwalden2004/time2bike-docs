Time2Bike uses invite links for four different things. The flow looks
similar but the destination differs.

## Organization invites

When an organizer invites you to join their team, you get an email with a
link to accept it.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click the link">
    If you're **not signed in** yet, Time2Bike sends you to
    [sign up](/riders/account/create-account) first (with the invite
    preserved). After you confirm your email, click **Accept Invitation**
    on the success page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm acceptance">
    If you're already signed in, the page accepts the invite automatically
    and drops you on your organizer
    [dashboard](/organizers/organization/dashboard-tour).
  </Step>
</Steps>


You now have whatever role the org admin assigned you: **viewer**,
**timer**, **finance**, **manager**, or **admin**. See
[Team & roles](/organizers/organization/settings-team) for what each role
can do.

## Team invites and join codes

A racing club can add you three ways:

- **An email invite** — same flow as above, but it drops you on the team
  instead of an organization.
- **A join code** — something like `ABCD-1234`, read out at practice or printed
  on a flyer. Redeem it at [time2.bike/join](https://time2.bike/join).
- **A join link** — `time2.bike/join/ABCD-1234`, which is the same thing with
  the code filled in.

Codes let you sign up **several riders at once** and set each person's role, so
a parent enrolling three kids does it in one pass. See
[Join a team](/teams/join-a-team).

## Participant invites (event registration)

If a friend or teammate registered you for a race, you'll get a
"You've been registered" email with a link to finish your half of it.

This is **not** an account invite. It's a "finish your part of the
registration" link. You'll:

1. Sign in or create an account.
2. Pick or create your participant profile.
3. Sign waivers.
4. Answer custom questions.
5. Click **Confirm registration**. There's no payment; it's already been paid
   for you.

See the [registration flow](/riders/registering/registration-flow#inviting-a-rider)
for details on the inviter's side.

## Participant manager invites

Someone can grant you access to their participant profile, for example
a parent giving their kid's profile to the other parent. Those invites come
in by email too, and the link opens that rider's **Access** page.

Accept the invite and the participant appears in your
[participants list](/riders/race-day/rider-profile) with the role
assigned (viewer / editor / manager).
