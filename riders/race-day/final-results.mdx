When the timing crew finishes checking a race, they **finalize** it and
**publish** it. The results page stops saying *provisional* and becomes the
official record.

It's the same page you were watching during the race —
`time2.bike/events/your-event/results`. Nothing moves, and any link you already
shared keeps working.

![Final results](/images/riders/results.png)

## What changes once they're official

| | While racing | Once published |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banner | *Live and provisional — times may change* | Nothing — the times are settled |
| Times | Can change as the crew fixes entries | Locked unless the organizer republishes |
| Places | Shuffle as riders finish | Final, with medals on the top three |

## Reading the table

Each race shows:

- **Place**, with a medal on the top three
- **Number** and **name**, plus the team the rider entered under
- **Time**
- **Gap** — how far behind the leader
- **Status** — finished, dnf, dns, or dq

Long start lists have a search box at the top: type a name, team, or number.

## Saving a copy

The **CSV** button on each race downloads that race's results as a spreadsheet
file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. Organizers can also publish a PDF
of the whole event.

## Brackets

Dual slalom and other bracket formats show the bracket above the table, so the
finished bracket stays readable after the event.

## Your own results

Every race you finish is saved to your rider profile, so you keep a running
record without doing anything.

→ [Your rider profile](/riders/race-day/rider-profile)

## If a result looks wrong

Contact the organizer. Everything the timing crew recorded is kept, including
every correction and who made it, so they can check what happened at the line
and republish if something needs fixing.
