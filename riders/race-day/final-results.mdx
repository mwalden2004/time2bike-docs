When the timing crew finishes checking a race, they **finalize** it and
**publish** it. The results page stops saying *provisional*. The organizer or
sanctioning body determines when results are official; corrections may still
require republication.

It's the same page you were watching during the race —
`time2.bike/events/your-event/results`, using the event's current web address.

## What changes once they're published

| | While racing | Once published |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Banner | *Live and provisional — times may change* | Provisional banner removed |
| Times | Can change as the crew fixes entries | Locked unless the organizer republishes |
| Places | Shuffle as riders finish | Published placing, with medals on the top three |

## Reading the table

Each race shows:

- **Place**, with a medal on the top three
- **Number** and **name**, plus the team the rider entered under
- **Time**
- **Gap** — how far behind the leader
- **Status** — finished, dnf, dns, or dq

Long start lists have a search box at the top: type a name, team, or number.

## Races that are scored in more than one group

One field can go down the course together and still award several sets of
places — a set per age bracket, per gender, or per bracket *and* gender. Where
an organizer has done that, the race is shown as one section per group, headed
the way the printed results sheet heads it (`40-49 · Female`). Place restarts at
1 in each section, and the gap is measured off that section's own leader.

Riders with no age bracket appear under **Ungrouped** and riders with no gender
on file under **Unspecified**, rather than being folded into a real group. Those
are the same words the printed sheet uses, so the page in your hand and the
sheet on the fence name the same group the same way.

## Show only…

When a race scores men and women apart, or splits by age bracket, filter chips
appear — at the top of the page for the whole event, and on each race.

- Every chip carries **its own count**, so you can see how big a group is before
  spending a tap on it.
- Counts are **cross-filtered**: pick a gender and the age chips count only
  riders of that gender, so the number on the chip is the number of rows you'll
  get.
- A race that couldn't contain what you filtered for — a men-only field, when
  you've asked for women — drops out rather than showing empty.

Filtering never renumbers anyone. Places are worked out over the whole field
first, so a rider who finished 14th reads 14th when you search their name, not
1st because they're the only row left.

The page-wide choice sets each race's, until you pick something different on a
particular race. Changing the page-wide choice resets those individual choices.

## Saving a copy

The **CSV** button on each race downloads that race's results as a spreadsheet
file you can open in Excel or Google Sheets. Organizers can also publish a PDF
of the whole event.

## Brackets

Dual slalom and other bracket formats show the bracket above the table, so the
finished bracket stays readable after the event.

## Your own results

Your rider profile shows linked registration history. Use the event's results

## If a result looks wrong

Contact the organizer with the race, rider name, number, and the issue you found.
They can review available timing records and correction history, then republish
if needed. Follow any correction or protest deadline set by the event.
