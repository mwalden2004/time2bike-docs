After the timing crew finalizes a race, official results post at
`time2.bike/events/[slug]/results`.

![Final results](/images/riders/results.png)

## What's different from live results

| | Live | Final |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **URL** | `/events/[slug]/live` | `/events/[slug]/results` |
| **Updates** | Every 30 seconds | Locked after publishing |
| **Status** | provisional | official |
| **Podium badges** | – | Gold / silver / bronze on top 3 |
| **Gap to leader** | – | Yes (e.g. `+0:42.123`) |

A race shows up here only after the organizer's timing crew clicks
**Finalize** and then **Publish** in the operator screen.

## Reading the table

Each race shows:

- **Rank** with a podium medal for 1st / 2nd / 3rd
- **Bib** number
- **Athlete name**
- **Finish time** in `h:mm:ss.ms` format
- **Gap** to the leader
- **Status** badge: finished / dnf / dns / dq
- **Publication** badge: provisional or official

## Downloading results

Click the **Download** button on a race table to get a CSV of that
race's official results. For a full PDF of every published session in
the event, organizers also publish a **Combined PDF** on the event page.

## Brackets

For dual slalom and other bracket formats, the bracket diagram appears
above the table, showing who beat whom round by round.

## If results look wrong

Final results come from the **event log** the timing crew recorded — every
button press, every CSV row from the RFID pad. If a result is wrong,
contact the organizer; they can edit and re-publish.
