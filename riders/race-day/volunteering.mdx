Many organizers run events with volunteers: course marshals, aid stations,
registration desk. Time2Bike has a built-in signup so you can claim a shift and
often earn a reward toward a future registration.

## Find a shift

There are two entry points:

- The **event page**. Look for **Volunteer** in the row of actions.
- The **Want to volunteer?** card on the event page, if shifts are still open.

Each event lists one or more **volunteer efforts** (e.g. "Race-day course marshals"). Pick one to see open shifts.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Pick a shift">
    Each shift shows the role, time window, and how many spots are left.
    Full shifts are marked **Full** and can't be selected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your info">
    Name, email, and phone. If you're logged in, these prefill. Add notes for the organizer if you want.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Accept the waiver">
    Read and accept each event waiver shown for volunteer signup. Racers may
    have additional race-specific waivers.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    You'll see a success page with the shift details and an option to download a calendar invite.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Rewards

Many volunteer efforts include a **reward**, emailed to you as a discount code
after the organizer marks your shift complete and issues the reward. Review the
effort's reward details before signing up and ask the organizer if they are unclear.

A reward is one of:

- **A free race entry** — a 100%-off entry credit.
- **A percentage off** one registration.
- **A dollar amount off** one registration.

Each reward states where it can be used: **this event** or **any of our races**
with that organizer. **This event** does not automatically include a future
edition. Ask the organizer where a code can be redeemed if the event has ended.

<Note>
  Rewards apply to eligible entry fees. License and plate fees are excluded
  unless the organizer includes them in the reward. Review the checkout total
  for any remaining fees or add-ons.
</Note>

Reward codes are single-use. Marked a **no-show** or cancelled your shift? No
code is issued.

## Cancelling

The confirmation email contains a single-use cancellation link if you can't make it. Use it early so the organizer can fill the spot.

## Race day

Show up at the time and location the organizer gave you and follow their role
instructions. They may scan a QR code or check you in by name. Ask the organizer
if a promised reward is missing after they confirm your completed shift.

If the organizer added **instructions** to your shift, covering what to bring,
where to check in and who to find, they're on the signup page and repeated in
your confirmation email, so you don't have to go hunting on race morning.
