Every event has one results page, and it starts filling in while the race is
still running. You'll find it at the event's **Results** link, or straight at
`time2.bike/events/your-event/results`.

![Race results](/images/riders/live-results.png)

There's no separate "live" page to find. The same page you'll read the official
results on is the one showing times right now. It just updates itself, then
settles down once the organizer signs the results off.

<Note>
  Results only appear while a race is on if the timing crew has switched
  **live results** on for that race. If a race is running and the page is empty,
  that's usually why. The times are being recorded, they're just not public yet.
</Note>

## What's on the page

- A **search box**. Type a rider's name, team, or number to pull them out of a
  long list.
- A count of how many riders are **on course** right now.
- One section per race, each with a table: place, number, name, team, and time.
- A **Gap** column showing how far each rider is behind the leader.

## Statuses

| You'll see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **on course** | Still out there, time still running |
| **finished** | Crossed the line with a recorded time |
| **dnf** | Started, didn't finish |
| **dns** | Didn't start |
| **dq** | Disqualified |

## While it's still live

A banner at the top says **Live and provisional, times may change**. Take that
seriously. A rider might be sitting in third and drop to fifth when the crew
sorts out a missed number.

- The page updates on its own. Leave it open; you don't need to refresh.
- A time can change or disappear if the crew fixes a mistake: a number typed
  wrong, a rider recorded in the wrong race. That's the system working.
- Places shuffle as riders finish, which is what you'd expect.

## Dual slalom and other bracket racing

Bracket races show the bracket diagram above the table, so you can follow who
beat whom round by round as the crew records each run.

![Dual slalom results](/images/riders/live-ds.png)

## When they become official

Once the timing crew finalizes and publishes, the **provisional** banner comes
off and the same page becomes the official record.
