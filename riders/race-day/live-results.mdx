An event's results page can show times while racing is underway when live
results are enabled. Find it at the event's **Results** link, or at
`time2.bike/events/your-event/results`.

<Frame caption="Demo data. Names, races, and results are fictional.">
  <img src="/images/riders/live-results.png" alt="Live results with fictional riders, finish times, and an on-course status" />
</Frame>

Live and published results use the same page. Live times are provisional;
the organizer or sanctioning body determines when results are official.

<Note>
  Results only appear while a race is on if the timing crew has switched
  **live results** on for that race. An empty page does not tell you whether
  times are being recorded. Ask the organizer if expected results are missing.
</Note>

## What's on the page

- A **search box**. Type a rider's name, team, or number to pull them out of a
  long list.
- A count of how many races are **on course** right now.
- One section per race, each with a table: place, number, name, team, and time.
- A **Gap** column showing how far each rider is behind the leader.

If the race is scored in more than one group — per age bracket, per gender, or
both — the table is split into a section per group, and filter chips let you
show only one of them. Places are worked out over the whole field before any of
that, so filtering never changes anybody's number.

→ [Races that are scored in more than one group](/riders/race-day/final-results#races-that-are-scored-in-more-than-one-group)

## Statuses

| You'll see | It means |
| --- | --- |
| **on course** | A start is recorded, with no finish recorded yet |
| **finished** | A finish time is recorded |
| **dnf** | Started, didn't finish |
| **dns** | Didn't start |
| **dq** | Disqualified |

These are timing statuses, not rider-location or safety tracking. Contact event
staff directly about a rider's whereabouts or an emergency.

## While it's still live

A banner at the top says **Live and provisional, times may change**. Take that
seriously. A rider might be sitting in third and drop to fifth when the crew
sorts out a missed number.

- The page refreshes results automatically while connected.
- A time can change or disappear if the crew fixes a mistake: a number typed
  wrong, a rider recorded in the wrong race. That's the system working.
- Places shuffle as riders finish, which is what you'd expect.

## Dual slalom and other bracket racing

Bracket races have **Head-to-head** and **Qualifying** tabs. Choose
**Head-to-head** to follow the bracket round by round as the crew records each run.

## When results are published

Once the timing crew finalizes and publishes, the **provisional** banner comes
off and the page shows published results. Publication alone does not establish
their official status; follow the organizer's or sanctioning body's announcement.
