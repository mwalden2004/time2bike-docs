Find these tools at `time2.bike/account/settings/privacy`.

![Privacy settings](/images/riders/privacy.png)

## Export your data

Click **Download my data** to get a file containing everything Time2Bike has
stored about you:

- Account profile
- Participant profiles you own
- Registrations
- Orders
- Waivers you've signed, and the exact wording you agreed to
- Results

The file is named with the date you asked for it, and it's a complete snapshot
of that moment. It's a data file rather than a document — if you just want a
receipt or a result to read, those are easier to find on your dashboard.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account:

- **Permanently removes** your account profile, rider profiles you
  own, login sessions, MFA devices, and stored payment methods.
- **Anonymizes** past results — your name is removed from public results
  pages, but the entries themselves stay (we can't remove someone from
  an official archived result).
- **Cancels** any pending registrations you haven't started. Already-paid
  registrations are not auto-refunded; contact the organizer.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Delete account">
    Open the delete dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type DELETE">
    Enter the literal word `DELETE` in the confirmation box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    Required to prove it's really you.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Time2Bike processes the deletion and signs you out.

    ![Delete confirmation](/images/riders/delete.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Deletion is irreversible — sign out and back in first if you just want
to clear sessions.
