## Export your data

Click **Download my data** to get a file with these account records:

- Account profile
- Rider profiles you own and profile-sharing access granted to your account
- Registrations purchased through your account
- Waiver signatures associated with rider profiles you own
- Phone numbers, sessions, and organization memberships

The export includes its generation date and omits passwords, session tokens,
and one-time codes. It does not include every type of record, such as team
medical notes or support messages. Contact support for data you need that is
missing from the download. For a receipt or result, start with your dashboard.

## Delete your account

Deleting your account:

- **Removes** your account record, sign-in sessions, saved phone numbers,
  organization memberships, and profile-sharing access granted to your account.
- **Changes rider profiles you own** to "Deleted User" and removes their
  account link, birthday, address, contact details, emergency contact, and
  license details. Registrations, results, and signed waivers are retained;
  deletion does not erase every copy of identifying information in those records.
- **Does not cancel or refund** registrations. Paid entries remain, with the
  purchasing-account link removed. Resolve cancellation or refund requests with
  the organizer before deleting your account.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Delete account">
    Open the delete dialog.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type DELETE">
    Enter the literal word `DELETE` in the confirmation box.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your password">
    Enter your current account password to authorize deletion.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm">
    Time2Bike processes the deletion and signs you out.

    <Frame caption="Expanded account-deletion confirmation with password and DELETE fields. Demo data.">
      <img src="/images/riders/delete.png" alt="Expanded account-deletion confirmation with password and DELETE fields." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
</Steps>

If you own an organization, transfer its ownership before deleting your account.
There is no self-service undo. To end sign-in sessions instead, use
[Account → Sessions](/riders/account/security-and-sessions).

For retention details or a request concerning remaining records, see the
[Privacy Policy](https://time2.bike/legal/privacy).
