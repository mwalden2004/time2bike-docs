Your **account profile** is who you are to Time2Bike — separate from
[rider profiles](/riders/race-day/rider-profile), which are the
racing identities tied to your account.

Find it at `time2.bike/account/settings`.

## Name and email

- **First Name** and **Last Name** — used on receipts and in dashboards
  for orgs you belong to. Click **Save Changes** after editing.
- **Email Address** — your sign-in email is read-only here. Contact support
  to change it.

## Phone numbers

You can add multiple phone numbers to your account. They power:

- **SMS multi-factor authentication** at sign-in
- **SMS notifications** from organizers (when supported)
- **Invite recovery** — if someone invites you by phone

### Adding a phone

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the number">
    Enter the phone in E.164 format (e.g. `+15551234567`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Send code">
    Time2Bike texts a 6-digit code. You have **3 minutes** before it
    expires.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter the code">
    Submit the code to verify. The phone moves to your **Verified
    phones** list.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Unverified phones can't be used for MFA or SMS notifications.

### Removing a phone

Click the **Trash** icon next to a phone to remove it. If that phone was
used for SMS MFA, that SMS method is disabled. Email MFA stays enabled if you
had turned it on. Set up a replacement SMS method in
[Security](/riders/account/security-and-sessions) with a different
phone.
