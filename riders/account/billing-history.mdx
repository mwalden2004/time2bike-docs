Time2Bike uses **Stripe** to collect online payments and manage saved payment
methods.

Find this page at `time2.bike/account/settings/billing`.

## Manage payment methods

Click **Manage in Stripe** to open the Stripe billing portal. From
there you can:

- Add or remove cards
- Update billing addresses
- Set a default payment method
- Download invoices

Time2Bike opens the portal in the same tab; when you're done, Stripe
sends you back to the site.

## Purchase history

Click **View History** under **Billing History** to open the Stripe billing
portal and review your invoices and payment history.

## Refunds

For an event-entry refund, contact the **organizer**, who applies the event's
refund policy. To request one:

1. Open the relevant event page.
2. Use the **Contact organizer** link.
3. Reference your registration ID (visible on the receipt email).

An approved refund must still be submitted and processed. Stripe normally
returns it to the original payment method; bank processing times vary, and a
refund can remain pending or fail. Contact the organizer if it has not arrived.

The refundable amount depends on the items and any prior refunds. Time2Bike's
service fee is generally non-refundable, with the exceptions described in the
[Terms of Service](https://time2.bike/legal/terms#refunds).
