Time2Bike uses **Stripe** for all payments. Your card details are stored
with Stripe, not with us.

Find this page at `time2.bike/account/settings/billing`.

![Billing settings](/images/riders/billing.png)

## Manage payment methods

Click **Manage in Stripe** to open the secure Stripe billing portal. From
there you can:

- Add or remove cards
- Update billing addresses
- Set a default payment method
- Download invoices

Time2Bike opens the portal in the same tab; when you're done, Stripe
sends you back to the site.

## Purchase history

The billing page also shows your past Time2Bike charges:

- Race registrations
- Merch orders
- Any other one-time purchases

Each row has the date, what you bought, and the total. Click into a row
for the line items and receipt PDF.

## Refunds

Refunds are handled by the **organizer**, not by Time2Bike directly. To
request one:

1. Open the relevant event page.
2. Use the **Contact organizer** link.
3. Reference your registration ID (visible on the receipt email).

If the organizer approves, the refund posts to your original payment
method through Stripe — usually within 5–10 business days.
