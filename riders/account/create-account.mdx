You need a Time2Bike account to register for any event on the platform.
Accounts are free, and a single account lets you register yourself, family
members, and teammates from one place.

## Sign up

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to the sign-up page">
    Visit [time2.bike/auth/register](https://time2.bike/auth/register), or
    click **Sign up** on any event page when prompted to register.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in your details">
    Enter:

    - **First name** and **Last name.** Use your legal name; it's what
      appears on start lists and results.
    - **Email.** Where waivers, receipts, and event updates are sent.
    - **Password** and **Confirm password.** Pick something you'll
      remember; you'll use it every time you sign in.

    ![Register Page](/images/riders/register.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Create account">
    Time2Bike emails you a confirmation link.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm your email">
    Open the email from Time2Bike and click the verification link. Your
    account isn't active until you do this, and you can't register for an
    event with an unconfirmed email.
    ![Inbox view of the "Confirm your email" email from Time2Bike](/images/riders/verify-account.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in">
    After confirming, click **Login** and [sign in](/riders/account/sign-in).
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Signing up from an invite

If an organization invited you (for example, a team owner adding you to a
team), the sign-up link includes the invite. After you confirm your email,
the page shows an **Accept Invitation** button that takes you straight into
the org or team.

See [Accepting an invite](/riders/invites/accept-invite) for details.

## Didn't get the confirmation email?

- Check spam / promotions.
- Make sure you typed your email correctly. If not, sign up again with the
  right address.
- Still nothing after a few minutes? Try [Forgot password](https://time2.bike/auth/forgot-password)
  with the email you used. That re-sends a verification email.
