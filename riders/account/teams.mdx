A **team** on Time2Bike is your racing club: a high-school squad, a junior
program, a shop team. It's separate from the organizations that put on events:
you race *for* your team, *at* an organization's event.

Your teams live at [time2.bike/teams](https://time2.bike/teams), or **Teams** in
the top navigation.

## Joining a team

Four ways in, depending on what your coach handed you:

| You were given | What to do |
|---|---|
| A code like `ABCD-1234` | Go to [time2.bike/join](https://time2.bike/join) and type it in |
| A link ending in `/join/…` | Open it |
| An email invite | Click the link in the email |
| A team page with **Apply to join** | Apply, and wait for an admin to approve |

Step-by-step: [Join a team](/teams/join-a-team).

<Tip>
  Signing up more than one rider, say three kids, or two kids and yourself as a
  coach? Do it in one pass. The join screen lets you tick several profiles at
  once and set each person's role individually.
</Tip>

## Your role on a team

You'll see two roles and they mean different things:

- **Athlete / Coach / Manager** — what you *do* on the team. This is picked when
  you join.
- **Owner / Admin / Member** — whether you can *change* the team. Most people are
  members.

Claiming coach or manager on a rider join code doesn't take effect until a team
admin confirms it. You'll be on the roster in the meantime.

## Racing under your team

Set a **default team** on your rider profile and it's applied automatically
every time you register, and shown next to your name in results.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your rider profile">
    From [time2.bike/profiles](https://time2.bike/profiles), click the rider
    you want to set a team on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Race Team card">
    It only appears once you belong to at least one team.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick your team, or 'Personal entry'">
    You can still remove it for a one-off entry during any single registration.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Not on a Time2Bike team? You can still type a club or sponsor name during
registration, and it's shown in results the same way.

More: [Racing together](/teams/racing-together).

## If your team races in a league

Joining a team that races under a league leaves you owing things: dues,
agreements, sometimes a division placement. Time2Bike shows you what's
outstanding right after you join, and again on the team's **League** tab.

Get it done before race day. A rider with unpaid dues or unsigned agreements can
be blocked from starting.

## Leaving a team

Open the team's **Members** page, find your own row (marked **You**), and click
**Take off roster**. You can always remove your own profiles.
