Sign in to access your registrations, sign waivers, view results, and manage
your profile.

## Sign in with email and password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the sign-in page">
    Go to [time2.bike/auth/login](https://time2.bike/auth/login), or click
    **Sign in** from the top navigation.

    ![Login Page](/images/riders/login.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your credentials">
    Type your **Email** and **Password**, then click **Sign in**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="(Optional) Complete MFA">
    If you've turned on multi-factor authentication, Time2Bike asks how you
    want to receive your one-time code:

    - **Text message** — sent to the phone number on your account.
    - **Email** — sent to your account email.

    Pick a method, click **Send code**, then enter the 6-digit code and
    click **Verify code**. Click **Resend** if it doesn't arrive.

    ![MFA Page](/images/riders/mfa.png)
  </Step>
</Steps>

After a successful sign-in you land on your dashboard, unless you were
mid-registration — in which case Time2Bike sends you back to where you
left off.

## Forgot your password?

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Forgot password">
    On the sign-in page, click **Forgot password?**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter your email">
    Submit the email on your account. Time2Bike sends a password-reset link
    if the address is registered.

    ![Forgot Page](/images/riders/forgot.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the email and reset">
    Click the link in the email — it opens a page where you set a new
    password.

    ![Reset Page](/images/riders/reset.png)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in with the new password">
    Old sessions are signed out automatically after a password change.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Verifying your email

If you didn't confirm your email when you signed up, click the link in your
welcome email. Once it's confirmed you can sign in normally.

## Managing active sessions

You can review every browser and device that's signed in to your account,
and sign them out individually, from
[Account → Sessions](/riders/account/security-and-sessions).

## Trouble signing in?

- **"Invalid email or password"** — double-check the email; if you have
  multiple, try them all.
- **"MFA code invalid"** — codes expire after a few minutes. Click
  **Resend** to get a fresh one.
- **Stuck on the MFA screen** — click **Back** to return to the password
  step.
- Still locked out? Email support from the help link at the bottom of the
  sign-in page.
