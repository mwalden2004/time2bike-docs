Step 2 of registration shows the waivers required for your entries. These may
include an organizer's liability waiver, a venue waiver, or a series-wide waiver.

## How signing works

For each participant and each waiver:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the waiver">
    The full text is visible on the page. Scroll through it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tick the statements, if there are any">
    Some sanctioning bodies require you to agree to specific statements
    separately from the signature — US Endurance asks for *"I agree to the
    terms & conditions of the Agreement."* Each appears as its own checkbox,
    and the exact wording is stored with your signature.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your initials">
    Type your initials in the box. Time2Bike records:

    - **Initials** you typed
    - **Timestamp** of signing
    - **Hash of the waiver content** to help identify the text associated
      with the signature if the document changes later
    - The **waiver version** in effect at signing time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign">
    The waiver flips to a green ✓ Signed state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't continue to the next step until every required waiver is signed
for every participant.

## Signing for someone else

Only sign for someone else if you have the authority required by the waiver.
Access to their rider profile or a team role does not establish that authority.

When a guardian signature is required, enter the parent or guardian's full name
and relationship to the rider. These fields record who is signing; completing
them does not by itself establish that a release is enforceable. Ask the
organizer if you are unsure who must sign.

If a participant is **invited** (registered by email/phone link), they
sign their own waivers from the
[complete-registration page](/riders/registering/registration-flow#inviting-a-rider),
not you.

## Signing at the desk on race morning

If you're entered by an organizer — a walk-up entry, or a waiver they flagged at
[check-in](/organizers/events/check-in) — you can sign there and then by typing
your full name on their tablet. Read each document before signing. Time2Bike
records the electronic signature and the official collecting it at the desk.

## Where to find your signed waivers

Organizers can review recorded waiver signatures in their dashboard. The
account export includes signatures associated with rider profiles you own.
Download it from
[Account → Privacy](/riders/account/privacy).
