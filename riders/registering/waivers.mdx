Step 2 of registration is signing waivers. Every race on Time2Bike has at
least one waiver attached — typically the organizer's liability waiver, a
venue waiver, or a series-wide waiver.

![Waiver page](/images/riders/waiver-1.png)

## How signing works

For each participant and each waiver:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Read the waiver">
    The full text is visible on the page. Scroll through it.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Type your initials">
    Type your initials in the box. Time2Bike records:

    - **Initials** you typed
    - **Timestamp** of signing
    - **Hash of the waiver content** so the organizer can prove what you
      signed even if the waiver text changes later
    - The **waiver version** in effect at signing time
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Sign">
    The waiver flips to a green ✓ Signed state.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can't continue to the next step until every required waiver is signed
for every participant.
![Waiver acceptance](/images/riders/waiver-2.png)

## Signing for someone else

If you're registering a minor or a teammate, you sign on their behalf.
The waiver presents a "Are you the participant?" toggle:

- **Yes** — recorded as the participant signing themselves.
- **No** — recorded as you signing as a parent/guardian or team captain.
  Your name (the account holder) is captured as the signer.

If a participant is **invited** (registered by email/phone link), they
sign their own waivers from the
[complete-registration page](/riders/registering/registration-flow#inviting-a-rider),
not you.

## Signing at the desk on race morning

If you're entered by an organizer — a walk-up entry, or a waiver they flagged at
[check-in](/organizers/events/check-in) — you can sign there and then by typing
your full name on their tablet. That's a real signature against the current text
of each document, recorded the same way as one made in checkout, and the
official who witnessed it is recorded too.

Two things follow from your date of birth, so it's worth having on your profile:

- **Which documents you're shown.** A waiver can be marked adults-only or
  minors-only, and you're only asked to sign the ones that apply to you.
- **Whether a parent or guardian signs instead of you.** Below the age of
  majority they do — and that isn't 18 everywhere: it's 19 in Alabama and
  Nebraska, 21 in Mississippi and Puerto Rico.

With no birthday on file, the desk asks for a guardian regardless, because a
minor's signature on a liability release isn't worth much.

## Where to find your signed waivers

After registration, every signed waiver is attached to your registration
record. Organizers can view them in their dashboard. You can re-download
your account data anytime from
[Account → Privacy](/riders/account/privacy).
