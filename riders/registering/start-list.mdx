Every event has a public start list — the **Who's registered** link on the
event page. It shows who's signed up, grouped by race.

![Whos Registered Page](/images/riders/whos-registered.png)

## What you see

For each race:

- Race name, category, and start time
- A count of how many riders are registered
- A table of riders: first name, last name, city, state, team (if any)

Email addresses, phone numbers, and birthdays are **not** public.

## Your row

After you complete registration, your name should appear on the start list
within a few seconds. If it doesn't:

1. Refresh the page.
2. Check your email for the receipt — if you got one, you're registered.
3. Make sure you completed the **Review & Pay** step (clicking back from
   Stripe without paying doesn't register you).

## Invited riders

If you invited a rider during registration, their row shows up as soon as
they claim their invite by signing waivers and picking a participant.
Until then, the spot is held but not displayed.

## Privacy

Organizers can choose to hide the public start list entirely. If the page
shows "Registrations are private", only the organizer can see who's
signed up.
