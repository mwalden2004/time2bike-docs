When the organizer enables a public start list, the **Who's registered** link
on the event page shows entries grouped by race.

## What you see

For each race:

- Race name, category, and start time
- A count of how many riders are registered
- A table of riders: first name, last name, city, state, team (if any)

Email addresses, phone numbers, and birthdays are **not included on the public
start list**.

## Your row

After you complete registration, check the public start list for your name.
If it doesn't appear:

1. Refresh the page.
2. Check your dashboard for the entry and its status, and check your email for
   the confirmation.
3. Make sure you completed the **Review & Pay** step (clicking back from
   Stripe without paying doesn't complete a paid entry).
4. Ask the organizer to confirm your entry if the status is unclear.

## Invited riders

If you invited a rider during registration, their row shows up as soon as
they claim their invite by signing waivers and picking a participant.
Until then, the spot is held but not displayed.

## Privacy

Organizers can choose to hide the public start list entirely. If the page
shows "Registrations are private", the public list is hidden. Authorized
organizer staff can still access registrations, and you can check your own
entries from your account.
