The final step of registration is **Review & Pay**. This is where the
order is finalized.

  <img src="/images/riders/discount.png" alt="Registration review showing rider details, a discount, and the final total" />

## What's on the review screen

- **Per-race line items**: race name, participant, price.
- **Add-ons / merch**: any extras you picked.
- **Discount**: if you applied a code, the discount line shows next to
  each affected race.
- **Service fee**: Time2Bike's platform fee.
- **Total**: what your card will be charged.

Double-check the participant names — that's what shows on the start list
and on results.

## Paying

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Pay">
    You'll be redirected to **Stripe Checkout** to enter (or pick) a
    payment method.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Complete payment">
    Stripe handles the card, Apple Pay, Google Pay, and any other methods
    the organizer has enabled. Enter your payment details on Stripe Checkout.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Land on the confirmation page">
    Once the payment goes through, you're taken to a confirmation page
    listing everything you just entered.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Confirmation email

After successful checkout, Time2Bike sends a receipt and confirmation to your
account email. Check your dashboard if the email is delayed or missing. The
confirmation includes:

- Event name, date, and venue
- Each race you registered for, with your participant name
- The total you paid
- A link back to the event page

## Holds & invites

After you pay for an invited rider, their entry remains **invited** until they
complete their part. Time2Bike sends a "Finish your registration" link. Ask the
organizer about an invite that remains unclaimed near race day.

Check your account for registration status. Invited riders appear on the
public [Who's registered](/riders/registering/start-list) page after completing
their registration, if the organizer makes that list public.

## Something went wrong?

- **Stripe declined the card** — try a different method, or contact your
  bank.
- **Page hung after Stripe** — check your dashboard and confirmation email
  before trying payment again. If the entry is missing or its status is unclear,
  contact the organizer with your participant name and receipt or payment reference.
- **Wrong participant** — contact the organizer to swap before race day.
