When you register for a race in a **series** that allows plate re-use, Time2Bike asks whether you want to **keep your existing plate** or **request a new one**.

You'll see this question on the **Review & pay** step, just above the discount
code field, with one row per rider on the order.

## Re-using your plate

The default. Pick this if you still have your assigned plate from a previous
race in the series. Your number carries forward without a replacement-plate
charge. Follow the organizer's check-in instructions and bring the plate.

## Requesting a new plate

Pick **Need a new plate** if you've lost or damaged your plate, or you're new to the series. The organizer's **replacement plate fee** (set by the series) is added to your order.

After payment, your registration appears on the organizer's **Bibs** page as a
**Plate request**. The organizer can then:

1. Assign you a new number (or confirm your old one).
2. Print or pull a plate for race-day check-in.
3. Mark the request complete.

You'll see the assigned number on your **Registration** page once they've finished.

## Cancellations and refunds

If the organizer can't fulfill the request, say because you cancel or the race
is called off, they can refund the replacement plate fee on its own. It doesn't
require a full registration refund.

## Where this is configured

- The **series** sets whether re-use is allowed and what the replacement fee is.
  See [Series plates](/organizers/series/series-plates).
- Individual **races** in the series inherit that policy when the organizer has
  set them to take plate numbers from the series. See
  [Bibs](/organizers/events/bibs).
