## How a race is priced

Each race on Time2Bike has a **price schedule** — a list of dated price
tiers. Common patterns:

- **Early bird** — $40 until 30 days before the race
- **Regular** — $55 from then until 2 days before
- **Race week** — $70 until registration closes

The price shown on the event page is **the tier in effect right now**,
not the cheapest one. When you check out, that's what you pay.

If the organizer changes the schedule mid-window, anyone who has already
paid keeps their old price. New registrations pay the current tier.

## Team races

For a **team race**, the price is **per team**, not per teammate. The
total you see on the event page is what the team pays once — split it
however you like with your teammates.

## Service fee

Time2Bike adds a small service fee on top of the race price, shown as a
separate line item on the Review step. The exact fee depends on the
organizer's plan.

## Discount codes

Organizers can issue **discount codes** that you enter at the Review
step.

![Discount code applied](/images/riders/discount.png)

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the code">
    Codes are case-insensitive. Enter it in the **Discount code** box and
    click **Apply**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Review the line items">
    If the code is valid, you'll see the discount applied per eligible
    race. If not, you'll see "Invalid discount code".
  </Step>
</Steps>

A discount can apply to:

- **Any event** — works on every race in your cart
- **A specific event** — works on races for that event only
- **A specific race** — works on that one race
- **A specific series** — works on any race that's part of that series

A single code can be set to apply to **race entries**, **merch & add-ons**, or **both** — so the same code might cover your entry fee, your jersey, or both, depending on how the organizer configured it.

If a code applies to only some of your races, you'll see it discount
those lines and skip the rest. The total updates automatically.

### Why a code might fail

- It's mistyped.
- The code has expired or hit its usage limit.
- It applies to a different event, race, or series than you're
  registering for.
- The organizer revoked it.

If you got a code from a sponsor or a series partner and it isn't
working, contact the organizer.

## Refunds & changes

Refund policy is set per-organizer. See
[Editing & refunds](/riders/race-day/rider-profile) on your profile, or
contact the organizer from the event page.
