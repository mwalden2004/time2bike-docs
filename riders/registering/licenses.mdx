Many sanctioned events require a valid racing **license**. Time2Bike checks for one during registration and offers a **one-day license** when the race allows it.

## When a license is required

When a permitted event's organizer marks a race **License required**, a license
panel appears on the **Review & pay** step. Requirements depend on the event
and race settings.

If the event isn't sanctioned, this whole flow is skipped.

## Already have an annual license

Enter your license number. For **USAC**, Time2Bike checks the issuer's public
license lookup. For other supported issuers, saving a number does not verify
its status with the issuer.

- A license recognized by checkout can remove the corresponding one-day fee.
  Review the license panel and total before paying.
- If verification fails, check the number and expiry, or choose a one-day
  license if the race offers one. Contact the organizer if neither option works.

Saved licenses can be reused from your rider profile. Keep the number and expiry
current, and bring any proof the organizer requires. A saved or verified number
does not establish eligibility for every category or event.

## Buy a one-day license

When the race allows one-day licenses, you can skip the annual license and pay the one-day fee instead. Pick the tier the organizer offers:

- **Beginner** — typically Cat 5 / novice events.
- **Experienced** — Cat 4 and up.

The fee is added to your order. The organizer handles any required filing with
the sanctioning body; contact them about the license terms or filing status.

<Note>
  A one-day license is offered for the specified event. Check the organizer's
  or issuer's terms for permitted categories and any limits; do not assume it
  applies to another event or provides particular insurance coverage.
</Note>

## UCI events

Time2Bike's UCI configuration requires an annual license and does not offer a
one-day option. Confirm the event's license and eligibility requirements with
the organizer.

## Where licenses appear later

- On your [rider profile](/riders/race-day/rider-profile), where you can maintain saved licenses.
- In the organizer's license report after the event, which they use to submit the one-day filings.
