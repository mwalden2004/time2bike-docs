Time2Bike is an end-to-end platform for bike racing. Organizers build event
pages, sell registrations and merch, run live timing on race day, and publish
official results. Riders sign up, sign waivers, get bib numbers, follow live
results, and keep a profile of every race they've finished. Clubs run their
rosters, schedule practices, and time their own athletes.

## The three words that get mixed up

<Columns cols={3}>
  <Card title="Organization" icon="building">
    Hosts events and takes entry fees.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Team" icon="people-group">
    A racing club riders belong to.
  </Card>
  <Card title="League" icon="landmark">
    A governing body teams race under.
  </Card>
</Columns>

They're separate, they don't share permissions, and you can be involved in all
three. Anything cross-cutting — slugs, Stripe, uploads, roles, SEO — is in
[Platform](/platform/slugs).
