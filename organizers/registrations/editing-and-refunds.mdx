## Edit a registration

The **Edit** action lets you change:

- **Race / category** — moves the rider; capacity in both source and target is updated.
- **Bib** — manual override.
- **Answers** to custom questions.
- **Emergency contact** / **address**.
- **Status** — manually flip to `cancelled` (does not issue a refund).

Saving sends a "registration updated" email to the rider unless you uncheck **Notify rider**.

## Refunds

Open the **Refund** action.

- **Full refund** — refunds the entire amount paid via Stripe to the rider's original payment method.
- **Partial refund** — refund any amount up to what was originally charged.
- **Reason** — internal note for your records.

The platform service fee is **not** refunded — on a partial refund or a full one. It is earned when the entry is taken. The only exception is a charge that should never have existed (a fraudulent charge, or a duplicate caused by a platform fault), where the whole charge including the service fee is returned.

The registration status flips to `refunded` and Time2Bike emails the rider a refund confirmation. The Stripe refund typically settles in 5–10 business days.

## When a rider asks to cancel

Riders can ask to cancel from their own dashboard. That is a message to you, not a refund: nothing changes on the entry until you act. Your refund terms decide the outcome.

Each open request shows up in three places on the Participants page:

- A **riders have asked to cancel** panel at the top, with the race, when they asked, their reason, a reply-to address and what is still refundable.
- An **Asked to cancel** pill on the rider's row.
- An **Asked to cancel** option in the status filter, so you can list just those rows.

You also get an email when the request comes in, with the same details.

To answer one:

- **Refund** from the panel or the row action. Refunding closes the request automatically and the rider's dashboard shows what came back.
- **Mark handled** when you are not refunding, or have already sorted it by email. You can leave a note; the rider sees it on their dashboard.

## Transfer

The **Transfer** action moves the registration's race entry from one rider account to another. Payments stay with the original payer; only the racer identity changes. Useful for selling/giving away a bib.

## Delete

Manager-only. Removes the registration record. The Stripe charge is **not** automatically refunded — issue the refund first if needed.
