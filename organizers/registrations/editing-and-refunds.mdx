## Edit a registration

Click the pencil icon (**Edit registration**) to change the race, status, recorded amount paid, internal notes, or team / club. Changing the status does not issue a refund.

You can also edit personal details for a profile that has not been claimed by an account. If a registered user owns the profile, the dialog explains that only that rider can change their personal details.

Click **Save Changes** to apply your edits. Edit a bib directly from the **Bib** cell in the table.

## Refunds

Open the **Refund** action.

- **Full refund** — submits the remaining refundable amount to the original
  payment method. This can be less than the total charge.
- **Partial refund** — enter an amount up to the remaining refundable balance.
- **Reason** — internal note for your records.

For ordinary registration refunds, the refundable portion is the entry fee and
one-day license fee, less discounts and earlier refunds. The platform service
fee is excluded. Other items, such as plate fees or league dues, may need a
separate refund process. Review the amount shown before submitting.

The [Terms of Service](https://time2.bike/legal/terms#refunds) describe full-charge
refund exceptions for confirmed fraud and duplicate or failed charges caused
by a platform fault. Contact support about those cases.

The registration changes to `refunded` when its refundable balance is exhausted.
A partial refund can leave an amount still refundable. Check the refund record
and Stripe payment status before telling the rider it is complete.

Bank processing times vary. Refunds can remain pending or fail; if a request
times out or its result is unclear, check Stripe before retrying. See
[Stripe's refund guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds).

## When a rider asks to cancel

Riders can ask to cancel from their dashboard. The request does not itself
refund or cancel the entry. Review it against your published refund terms and
any applicable requirements, then record your response.

Each open request shows up in three places on the Participants page:

- A **riders have asked to cancel** panel at the top, with the race, when they asked, their reason, a reply-to address and what is still refundable.
- An **Asked to cancel** pill on the rider's row.
- An **Asked to cancel** option in the status filter, so you can list just those rows.

You also get an email when the request comes in, with the same details.

To answer one:

- **Refund** from the panel or the row action. Refunding closes the request automatically and the rider's dashboard shows what came back.
- **Mark handled** when you are not refunding, or have already sorted it by email. You can leave a note; the rider sees it on their dashboard.

## Transfer

The **Transfer** action moves the registration's race entry from one rider account to another. Payments stay with the original payer; only the racer identity changes. Confirm that the transfer is permitted under your event's rules.

## Delete

Manager-only. Removes the registration record. The Stripe charge is **not** automatically refunded — issue the refund first if needed.
