**Add entry** on the [Event registrations](/organizers/registrations/event-registrations)
page registers a rider on their behalf. The same form runs the **Walk-up
Registration** button on the [Check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) — the
hard part is identical either way, and only the pricing and what happens
afterwards differ.

| Where you opened it | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Participants → Add entry** | Tidying the roster at any point in the season |
| **Check-in → Walk-up Registration** | Race morning: registration has shut, somebody is standing there |

## Three modes

Pick the one that matches who you're adding:

- **Existing participant** — the rider already has a profile with your
  organization. Search and attach, rather than creating their second profile.
- **New participant** — full details, typed in.
- **Invite** — you only have an email or phone. Time2Bike creates a pending
  registration and invites the rider to finish their own details and waivers.

One submission can enter the rider in **several races** at once.

## What the form asks

| Section | Fields |
|---|---|
| **Rider** (new mode) | First/last name, gender, birthday, address, contact, emergency contact |
| **Invite** (invite mode) | Email and/or phone, optional name |
| **Races** | One or more. Capacity-checked |
| **Waivers** | Sign here, or record that a paper form was signed |
| **Licence** | Add an existing one, sell a one-day, or skip |
| **Payment** | Amount, paid or pending, notes — or take the money now |

## Relay teams

Picking a relay race turns the rider section into one slot per leg, plus a team
name — the same full form repeated, deliberately.

The form collects details and waiver responses separately for each member.
A team captain's entry does not supply another member's signature.

## Waivers

For a non-invite entry, complete the required waiver fields for the selected
races. Review the documents shown and choose how signing is recorded:

- **Sign here** — collects signature details for the current version of each
  document that applies to them. Depending on your
  [desk signing setting](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers#signing-at-the-desk),
  either you type the name the rider gives you and confirm any statements the
  waiver requires, or you hand them the tablet to read and sign themselves.
- **Signed on paper** — records that you hold the form. It doesn't create a
  signature document, because the paper form is the record.

Which documents a rider signs, and whether a parent has to sign instead, depend
on the stored birthday and waiver policy. Where that policy requires a
guardian, the form requires the guardian's name; it does not verify their
authority to sign.

**Invite** mode is the way to add a rider who isn't in front of you: nothing
is signed now, and the rider signs their own waivers when they complete the
registration from the link.

→ [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers)

## Racing licences

Choose how to handle the rider's licence when making the entry:

- **Add an existing licence** — USA Cycling numbers use an online lookup.
  Other issuers currently have no live verification; review the issuer's
  requirements separately.
- **Sell a one-day** — priced from the event's own rate, and it lands on the
  licence-filing list.
- **Skip** — leave licensing to be checked separately. This does not confirm
  that the rider is eligible.

→ [Licences & one-day fees](/organizers/events/licenses)

## Money

**Amount paid** applies to every race, or you can override it per race, and the
entry is marked **Paid** or **Pending** with an optional note ("cash at gate").
Nothing is charged — you're recording money that moved elsewhere.

**Take payment now** uses the organization's connected Stripe account. Review
the amount and fee shown, then check the payment result before treating the
entry as paid. The option is available from the check-in desk and registrations
page.

→ [Taking money at the desk](/organizers/events/check-in#taking-money-at-the-desk)

### Comping an entry

**Comp** gives the entry away and asks why — a volunteer, a sponsor, the friend
who drove the moto. It's not the same as typing an amount of zero: a comp
records the decision and captures what the entry would have cost, so "what did
we give away this season" has an answer that a zero-priced registration would
not show.

At the desk, comping has to be allowed on the race first; the button says so
when it isn't.

## What the desk adds

Opened from check-in, the form also:

- **Quotes a day-of price per race**, from the race's day-of rate where one is
  set, otherwise from whatever price tier resolves today. Races are listed with
  the group they belong to, because event races reuse names across groups.
- **Shows registration warnings** — such as a closed window, full race, or
  existing waitlist — and records the operator's acknowledgement. This does
  not waive requirements set by your event or sanctioning body.
- **Tags the entry as a walk-up**, so it stays findable when a permit headcount
  or a revenue figure looks wrong afterwards.
- **Hands over a plate number** straight afterwards, suggesting the plate the
  rider already resolves to (a season plate, or one assigned earlier at this
  event) or the next free number.

## What happens afterwards

- The registration is created with the operator recorded as the source.
- Invite mode emails or texts the rider to finish their details and waivers.
- Manual entries count toward capacity exactly like web registrations.
- Amounts logged (rather than charged) appear in revenue analytics as manual
  entries.
