`Event → Registrations` is the most-used organizer screen. It shows every registration across every race in one filterable table.

## Columns

- **Athlete** — name, gender, age bucket.
- **Email**, **phone**.
- **Race / category**.
- **Bib**.
- **Amount paid**.
- **Status** — paid / pending / cancelled / refunded.
- **Source** — whether the rider signed up themselves, someone on your team
  entered them by hand, or the entry came in from another system.
- **Registered at**.

Rows placed while the event was a draft carry a **Test** badge. They're dry runs
by your own staff, they took no money, and they should be cleared before you
publish — see [Test entries](/organizers/events/test-entries).

Use the column header to sort. The search box filters by name, email, or bib.

## Row actions

Click a row to open the registration detail panel:

- **Custom question answers** (see [Custom questions](/organizers/events/questions)).
- **Waiver signatures** with version, initials, signed-at, IP, and content hash.
- **Emergency contact** and **address** (if collected).
- **Payment timeline** with discount codes applied.

From the panel you can:

- **Edit** — open the [EditRegistrationModal](/organizers/registrations/editing-and-refunds) to change race, category, answers.
- **Transfer** — move the registration to a different rider's account.
- **Refund** — see [Editing and refunds](/organizers/registrations/editing-and-refunds).
- **Resend confirmation email**.
- **Delete** — manager-only.

## Export

**Download CSV** exports the current filtered view with all columns plus question answers expanded into columns.

## Bulk actions

Select rows with the checkboxes for bulk **email**, **delete**, or **export selected**.

## Clear entries

**Clear entries** in the toolbar empties the event. Time2Bike counts what's
there and shows you the numbers before deleting anything.

| Scope | What it removes | Who can run it |
|---|---|---|
| **Test entries only** | Just the dry runs placed against the draft. No real rider is touched. | Admin or manager |
| **Everything** | Every entry on the event, imports and paid entries included. Asks you to type the event name back. | Organization owner |

<Warning>
  Clearing is permanent and does **not** refund anybody. To give people their
  money back, run **Refund all** first — see
  [Editing and refunds](/organizers/registrations/editing-and-refunds).
</Warning>

Waiver signatures, guardian consents, discount redemptions, league memberships,
start-list entries, and emptied relay teams go with the registrations. Plate
assignments are kept. Full detail in
[Test entries](/organizers/events/test-entries).
