A **Time2Bike organization** is the workspace your race crew uses to publish
events, sell registrations and merch, run live timing, and publish results.

If you're setting one up for the first time, go straight to the
[Quickstart](/organizers/quickstart) — it's the whole path from empty account to
open registration in one page. This page is the map.

## Organization, team, league

Three words that get mixed up. They're separate, and they don't share
permissions.

| | What it is | You want one if… |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization** | The workspace that *hosts* races | You put on events and take money for entries |
| **Team** | A racing club riders belong to | You coach a squad — [see Teams](/teams/overview) |
| **League** | A governing body teams race under | You run a season across multiple clubs |

You can be all three. They're managed separately.

## What's inside an organization

- **Dashboard** — recent activity, upcoming events, revenue.
- **Events** — registration pages, races, questions, waivers, bibs, check-in,
  timing, results, analytics.
- **Registrations** — who's entered, manual entry, edits and refunds.
- **Pricing** — price schedules, discount codes, redemptions, refunds.
- **Merch** — products, variants, orders.
- **Series** — multi-event championships with shared plates.
- **Volunteers** — opportunities, shifts, rewards, race-day check-in.
- **Imports** — bulk import contacts, registrations, and results from another
  platform.
- **Settings** — org profile, team, sponsors, waivers, Stripe payouts.

## Roles inside an organization

Invite teammates from **Settings → Team**. A user can belong to many
organizations with a different role in each.

| Role | What it's for |
|---|---|
| **Viewer** | Read-only access to org data. |
| **Timer** | Manage bib assignments and race timing. |
| **Finance** | Manage billing and payouts. |
| **Manager** | Create and edit events, races, pricing, and merch. |
| **Admin** | Full access, including team management. |

The user who creates the organization becomes its **owner**.

<Note>
  Roles preselect permissions. Review those permissions and each member's
  event access when inviting people or changing their role. See
  [Team & invites](/organizers/organization/settings-team).
</Note>

For race day, you can hand out narrow, per-event timing links instead of org
access — see [Who can do what](/timing/crew/roles).

## Where money moves

Time2Bike uses **Stripe Connect**. Riders pay through Stripe Checkout and funds
are processed through your connected Stripe account. Payout availability and
timing depend on that account's status and Stripe's processing. Connect it
under **Settings → Billing**.

You cannot publish a paid event before Stripe is connected. See
[Stripe Connect](/platform/stripe-connect).
