A **Time2Bike organization** is the workspace your team uses to publish events, sell registrations and merch, run live timing, and publish results.

Most organizers work through these surfaces:

- **Organization dashboard** — recent activity, upcoming events, revenue.
- **Events** — registration pages, races, questions, waivers, bibs, timing, results, analytics.
- **Pricing** — schedules, discount codes, redemptions, refunds.
- **Merch** — products, variants, orders.
- **Series** — multi-event championships with shared plates.
- **Settings** — org profile, team, sponsors, waivers, Stripe payouts.

## Roles inside an organization

Invite teammates from **Settings → Team**. Each member has one of:

- **Viewer** — read-only access to events, registrations, and analytics.
- **Editor** — create and edit events, races, pricing, merch.
- **Manager** — everything editors can do, plus billing, team, and series.

The user who creates the org is automatically the first manager.

## Where money moves

Time2Bike uses **Stripe Connect**. Riders pay through Stripe Checkout and funds settle into your connected Stripe account on Stripe's normal payout schedule. You connect Stripe once under **Settings → Billing**; nothing else collects rider payments.
