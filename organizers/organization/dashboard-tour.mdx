The **organization dashboard** is the landing page for every org member. Visit it at `/organizations/[id]`.

[IMAGE: /organizations/[id] → full dashboard with stat cards, upcoming events, recent activity]

## Stat cards

Across the top:

- **Total events** and **active events**
- **Upcoming events** (published and starting in the future)
- **Total registrations** and **recent registrations** (last 30 days)
- **Total revenue** (net, after refunds)
- **Unique contacts** that have ever registered
- **Series** count

Numbers come straight from your registrations and merch orders — no aggregation lag.

## Upcoming events

A list of your next-up published events. Click any row to jump to the **Event overview**. Use **Create event** in the header to add a new one.

## Recent activity

The latest 10–15 registrations across the whole org with a status pill:

- **Paid** — payment confirmed.
- **Pending** — checkout started, not yet completed.
- **Cancelled** / **Refunded** — see the [Refunds page](/organizers/pricing/refunds).
