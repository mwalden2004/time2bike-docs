The **organization dashboard** is the first thing every member of your
organization sees. Pick your organization from
[time2.bike/organizations](https://time2.bike/organizations) to open it.

![Organization dashboard](/images/orgs/tour.png)

## Stat cards

Across the top:

- **Total events** and **active events**
- **Upcoming events** (published and starting in the future)
- **Total registrations** and **recent registrations** (last 30 days)
- **Total revenue** (net, after refunds)
- **Unique contacts** that have ever registered
- **Series** count

Numbers come straight from your registrations and merch orders — no aggregation lag.

## Upcoming events

A list of your next-up published events. Click any row to jump to the **Event overview**. Use **Create event** in the header to add a new one.

## Recent activity

The latest 10–15 registrations across the whole org with a status pill:

- **Paid** — payment confirmed.
- **Pending** — checkout started, not yet completed.
- **Cancelled** / **Refunded** — see the [Refunds page](/organizers/pricing/refunds).

## Getting around

Everything your organization can do is in the sidebar (a dropdown on a phone):

| Section | What's there |
|---|---|
| **Overview** | This page |
| **Events** | Every event you've created, and **Create event** → [Create an event](/organizers/events/create-event) |
| **Recurring races** | Races that run more than once, their permanent addresses and results archives → [Races that run again](/organizers/events/recurring-races) |
| **Series** | Multi-event championships and season passes → [Series](/organizers/series/create-series) |
| **Bibs** | Numbers across the whole organization → [Bibs](/organizers/events/bibs) |
| **Contacts** | Everyone who has ever registered → [Contacts](/organizers/organization/contacts) |
| **Imports** | Bringing registrations, results and contacts over from another platform → [Imports](/organizers/organization/imports) |
| **Pricing** | Price schedules, discount codes, redemptions and refunds → [Price schedules](/organizers/pricing/price-schedules) |
| **Merch** | Products and orders → [Products](/organizers/merch/products) |
| **Analytics** | Registrations and revenue across the org → [Analytics](/organizers/organization/analytics) |
| **Settings** | Details, team, billing, messaging, waivers, sponsors and age groups → [Settings](/organizers/organization/settings-details) |
