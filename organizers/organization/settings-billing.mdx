Time2Bike uses **Stripe Connect** for online registration and merch payments.
Connect your organization's Stripe account to collect payments and manage
payouts. Review Stripe's requirements and any tax documents available for your
account; connecting Stripe does not determine your tax obligations.

## Connect your Stripe account

1. Open **Settings → Billing**.
2. Click **Connect Stripe**.
3. Complete Stripe's onboarding (business info, bank account, identity verification).
4. Return to Time2Bike and check the connection status. Complete any outstanding
   Stripe requirements before accepting payments.

If your account is not fully verified, Stripe may hold payouts until you finish onboarding. Click **Connect Stripe** again to pick the onboarding flow back up where you left it.

## After connecting

- **Open Stripe dashboard** opens your own Stripe dashboard in a new tab for payouts, statements, and tax docs. You sign in with the Stripe login you created during onboarding.
- Online card payments are charged to your connected Stripe account. Cash,
  waived, and test entries do not create a Stripe payment.
- Refunds are issued by Time2Bike against the original charge — see [Refunds](/organizers/pricing/refunds).
- Any platform service fee is shown separately before purchase and on the
  receipt. Stripe processing fees, refunds, disputes, and other adjustments
  affect your net payout.

You can publish **free** events without connecting Stripe. The connect step is only required once you charge for anything.
