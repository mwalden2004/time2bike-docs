**Settings → Sponsors** holds a single library of sponsor logos that you can show across your public org page and any of your event pages.

![Sponsors settings page](/images/orgs/sponsors.png)

## Add a sponsor

1. Click **Add sponsor**.
2. Fill in **name**, **website**, and upload a **logo** (PNG with transparency preferred).
3. Pick **visibility**:
   - **All events** — appears on every event page and your org page.
   - **Specific events** — choose events from a multiselect.
   - **Org only** — appears on your public org page, not on event pages.
4. Toggle **active** off to temporarily hide without deleting.

Riders see sponsors in the footer of your public pages and in event landing-page sponsor grids.

## Per-event control

This page is your library and your organization-wide default. What any single
event actually shows is decided on that event's own **Sponsors** tab, which can
take **all** of your org sponsors, **none** of them, or a **selected** few — and
can add sponsors that belong to that event alone.

That's the screen to use when you're timing someone else's race, or when one
event has its own title sponsor. It never edits the sponsors here and never
affects another event.

→ [Event sponsors](/organizers/events/event-sponsors)

<Note>
  A sponsor set to **Org only** here still appears in an event's picker. An
  organizer can tick it there as a deliberate override for that one event.
</Note>

League sponsors are separate again: if your event is a league round, the
league's sponsors always appear and can't be dropped.
