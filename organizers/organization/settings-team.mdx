**Settings → Team** lists every member of your organization and every outstanding invite.

![Team settings page](/images/orgs/team.png)

## Invite a teammate

1. Click **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **email** and pick a **role**:
   - **Viewer** — read-only access to org data.
   - **Timer** — manage bib assignments and race timing.
   - **Finance** — manage billing and payouts.
   - **Manager** — create and edit events, races, pricing, and merch.
   - **Admin** — full access.
3. Click **Send invitation**.

The invitee gets an email with a one-time link. When they accept (see [Accept an invite](/riders/invites/accept-invite)) they join immediately.

<Warning>
  **While Time2Bike is in beta, every team member has full access regardless of
  the role you pick.** The role is stored and will be enforced later, but it
  restricts nothing today. Only invite people you'd be comfortable making an
  admin.

  For race-day volunteers, issue a [timing link](/timing/crew/tokens-and-qr)
  instead of an org invite — those really are limited to the one event.
</Warning>

## Manage existing members

- **Remove member** revokes access. They keep their personal Time2Bike account, but lose this org.
- **Revoke invite** cancels a pending invite. Resend by inviting again.
- **Resend invite** re-sends the email to someone who lost the link.

Roles are chosen at invite time. To change someone's role, remove them and
invite them again with the role you want.

The organization's owner can't be removed from the team list.
