**Settings → Team** lists every member of your organization and every outstanding invite, with what each one can do.

## Invite a teammate

1. Click **Invite member**.
2. Enter their **email**.
3. Pick a **role**. It fills in the permissions for you:
   - **Admin** — everything, including the team, billing and org settings.
   - **Manager** — runs events end to end. No team, billing or org settings.
   - **Finance** — billing, pricing, refunds, merch orders and analytics.
   - **Timer** — race day: live timing, results, check-in and bibs.
   - **Check-in desk** — check riders in, take walk-ups, hand out bibs.
   - **Viewer** — read-only.
4. Choose **event access**:
   - **All events** — everything you run, now and later.
   - **Only selected events** — limit their organizer access to those events.
5. Optionally open **Permissions** and tick or untick anything. The role then shows as **Custom**.
6. Click **Send invitation**.

The summary line at the bottom of the dialog always shows what you are about
to grant — for example *Timer · 2 events · 6 permissions*.

The invitee gets an email naming the role and events. When they accept (see
[Accept an invite](/riders/invites/accept-invite)) they join immediately with
exactly that access. You can change a pending invite from the list before it
is accepted.

<Tip>
  A volunteer who only works one race day is a good fit for **Check-in desk**
  or **Timer** limited to that one event. For someone who should never need to
  sign in at all, a [timing link](/timing/crew/tokens-and-qr) still works and
  is limited to a single timing session.
</Tip>

<Note>
  Event access limits which events a person can open. It does **not** narrow
  organization-wide permissions such as Contacts, Billing or Series — the
  dialog warns you when you combine the two. See
  [Roles & permissions](/platform/roles-and-permissions) for the full list.
</Note>

## Change what someone can do

Click the **pencil** next to a member or a pending invite. The same dialog
opens with their current role, events and permissions filled in. Save to
apply; the change takes effect on their next request.

You cannot edit your own access — ask the owner or another admin.

## What you can grant

You can only give people what you hold yourself. A manager cannot invite an
admin, and someone limited to two events cannot grant a third. Anything you
cannot grant appears locked in the dialog. The owner can grant everything.

## Remove someone

- **Remove member** removes this membership's access on subsequent requests.
  It does not erase information they already viewed or exported, and they keep
  their personal Time2Bike account.
- **Cancel invitation** stops a pending invite; the link in the email stops
  working.
- **Resend invitation** sends the email again to someone who lost it.

The organization's **owner** always has full access and cannot be edited or
removed from this page. A site admin can transfer ownership if needed.
