**Contacts** is a roll-up of every unique person who has registered for any of your events. Use it to review registration history, find contact details, and export selected records.

Open **Contacts** from your organization's menu.

## What's in the table

| Column | Notes |
|---|---|
| Name | From the rider's most recent registration. |
| Email | Primary contact email. |
| Phone | Optional. |
| Registrations | Count across all your events. |
| Total spent | Lifetime, gross, in your default currency. |
| First / last registration | Dates of first and most recent registration. |

## A contact's full history

Click any row to open everything that person has done with your organization, in one place:

- **Timeline** — every event below merged into one newest-first list.
- **Entries** — each race they registered for, with the event, race, category, plate, what they paid, any discount code, refunds, check-in and waiver status.
- **Results** — place in category and overall, time, and DNF/DNS/DQ status, with podiums and wins counted at the top.
- **Coupons** — volunteer credits awarded to them and every discount code they have used, with what it saved.
- **Messages** — every email and text broadcast that went to one of their addresses, with who sent it and a preview. A message is matched by the email or phone number on the send, so a rider who changed their address after a send will not show that older message.
- **Volunteering** — shifts they signed up for, with role, time and status.
- **Orders** — merchandise they bought, and how the order was linked to them (through a registration, their account, or their email).
- **Records** — refunds, signed waivers, plate assignments, waitlist places and staff actions logged against the record.

Merged participant records are folded in automatically, and the header says how many were combined.

The same view opens from a row on an event's
[Participants](/organizers/registrations/event-registrations) page, with that
race's entry pinned at the top.

## Search & filter

Type in the search box to filter by name, email, or phone. Filters apply live.

## Export

Click **Export CSV** to download the current view. If you've selected specific rows with the checkboxes, only those are exported; otherwise you get every visible row.

Headers in the export: Name, Email, Phone, Registrations, Total Spent, First Registration, Last Registration.

Review communication preferences before adding exported contacts to a marketing
list. A contact appearing here does not itself record permission to receive
marketing messages.
