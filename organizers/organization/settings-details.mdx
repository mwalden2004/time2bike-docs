**Settings → Details** controls everything that appears on `time2.bike/[slug]` — your public organization page.

![Details page](/images/orgs/details.png)

## Brand & description

- **Logo** — uploaded square image; used in the nav, public page, emails.
- **Description** — short blurb shown under your name in search results and event headers.
- **About** — long-form markdown for the body of your public page.
- **Public website** — outbound link in your public profile.
- **Merch store URL** — optional external storefront link if you sell merch off-platform.

## Social links

Facebook, Instagram, Strava, X, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn. Each renders as an icon in the public page header.

## Custom links

Add arbitrary `label → URL` rows for things like rule books, sponsor pages, or club resources. They render as a button group on your public page.

## FAQ

Question/answer pairs that render as an accordion on your public page. Useful for "Where do I park?", "What's the refund policy?", etc.

## Featured content

Toggle individual **events** and **series** as featured to pin them above the chronological list. You can also hide **upcoming** or **past** event sections entirely.
