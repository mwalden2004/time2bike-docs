**Settings → Details** controls everything that appears on your public
organization page.

![Details page](/images/orgs/details.png)

## Brand & description

- **Logo** — uploaded square image; used in the nav, public page, emails.
- **Description** — short blurb shown under your name in search results and event headers.
- **About** — long-form markdown for the body of your public page.
- **Public website** — outbound link in your public profile.
- **Merch store URL** — optional external storefront link if you sell merch off-platform.

## Social links

Facebook, Instagram, Strava, X, YouTube, TikTok, LinkedIn. Each one shows as an
icon at the top of your public page.

## Custom links

Add your own links — a rule book, a sponsor, a club resource. Give each one a
label and a web address, and they appear as a row of buttons on your public
page.

## FAQ

Questions and answers, shown on your public page as a list riders can expand.
Useful for "Where do I park?" and "What's the refund policy?".

## Featured content

Toggle individual **events** and **series** as featured to pin them above the chronological list. You can also hide **upcoming** or **past** event sections entirely.
