---
title: "Teams (riders' clubs)"
description: "How rider teams relate to your organization, and what you see of them."
---

**Teams** and **organizations** are different things on Time2Bike:

- An **organization** is your workspace as an event host — the thing you're in
  right now.
- A **team** is a racing club a rider belongs to.

They're stored separately and share no permissions. A rider on Coldwater
Composite can race events run by any organization; their team affiliation just
travels with their name.

## What you see

Team affiliation shows up next to a rider's name across the platform:

- On the **start list** and public registration list.
- On your **registrations** tables.
- In **results**, published and live.

Riders attach it themselves — either from a **default team** set on their rider
profile, or picked during registration. Riders who aren't on a Time2Bike team can
type a free-text club or sponsor name instead, and that's shown the same way.

<Note>
  You can't edit a rider's team, and a team can't edit your event. If an
  affiliation is wrong, the rider fixes it on their own profile — or you can
  correct the registration itself. See
  [Editing registrations](/organizers/registrations/editing-and-refunds).
</Note>

## Team admins registering their riders

A team owner or admin can register several of their members for one of your
races in a single checkout, paying once. From your side this looks like an
ordinary registration — one order, several participants, all tagged with the
same team.

Members have to opt in to this individually, so you'll never see a team
registering someone who didn't agree to it.

See [Racing together](/teams/racing-together).

## Team-versus-team scoring

Scoring whole teams against each other is a **league** feature, not an event
setting — there is no per-race team scoring toggle. If you're running a
championship where clubs compete against clubs, that lives in the league tools.

For individual season-long standings across several of your events, use a
[series](/organizers/series/series-overview) instead.

## Relay teams are something else

The **relay team** you build inside the registration flow is scoped to a single
race. It shares the word "team" and nothing else — it isn't a club, has no
roster, and disappears with the event.
