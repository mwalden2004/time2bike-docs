## Start from a template

**New waiver → Start from** offers templates, including the US Endurance
specific-event release. Choosing one fills in its text, version, audience,
and signing statements, with issuer requirements shown beside the form.

Confirm the current wording, signing process, and record-retention requirements
with the issuer before using a template for your event. Selecting a template
does not establish that your event meets those requirements.

## Create a waiver

1. Click **New waiver**.
2. Set:
   - **Title** — what riders see.
   - **Version** — a label such as `2027.1`, recorded with signatures.
   - **Applies to** — **Adults**, **Minors**, or **Everyone**. See
     [Who signs](#who-signs-riders-and-guardians) for how this affects signing.
   - **Content** — the markdown text presented for signing. Use
     `{{event.name}}` and `{{event.dates}}` to insert event details.
   - **Statements to tick** — separate lists for riders and guardians.
     These appear as required checkboxes, and the submitted statements are
     recorded with the signature.
3. Click **Save**.

### Attaching the original PDF

Use **Attach PDF** to provide the issuer's document alongside the waiver text.
Signing screens offer a **View the original PDF** link. The text shown in the
signing screen is the content associated with the signature; the PDF is a
separate reference. Check that the two agree before collecting signatures.

## Who signs: riders and guardians

The signing form uses the rider's date of birth and the waiver's guardian
policy to decide whether to request guardian details. The age-based policy uses
the waiver's configured state, or the organization's mailing state when none is
set. Missing birthdays can trigger a guardian request; collect the birthday
and review the documents shown.

These are the platform's collection rules. They do not determine whether a
release is enforceable or whether a person has authority to sign for a rider.
Confirm the signing requirements for your event when preparing its documents.

### Where this is applied today

The [check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) and
[manual entry](/organizers/registrations/manual-entry) filter documents using
**Applies to** and the rider's age. Where the configured guardian policy requires
one, the form requires a guardian's name. League signing also uses guardian
policies.

Riders registering through checkout are currently shown every waiver attached
to the race. If you use separate adult and minor documents, review both the
online checkout and desk flows before opening registration.

## Signing at the desk

**Settings → Waivers → Signing at the check-in desk** controls the information
the desk asks for. Choices save as you select them:

| Setting | Options | What happens |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Signature** | Typed by the official / Drawn by the rider | The official enters the supplied name, or hands over the device for the signer to read the document, enter a name, and draw a signature. |
| **Photo at signing** | No photo / Offered / Asked for | The signing screen offers a photo capture. **Asked for** can still be skipped, with the reason recorded. |

**Preview what the rider sees** opens a preview using your waiver text. Preview
signatures and photos are not submitted as registration records.

### What the rider sees

The signer reviews the document, records that they have read it, completes the
signature fields, and taps **Hand back**. The official then reviews the captured
information before submitting it. Where guardian details are required, the
screen asks for them too.

Choose the signature and photo options that fit your event's signing process.
Explain any photo request to the signer and use the skip option when needed.

### What is stored

Signature details include the version, signing time, supplied name, and a hash
of the waiver text. Depending on the signing method and what was collected,
they can also include initials, guardian details, the operator, IP address,
drawn strokes, read confirmation, a photo, or a reason a photo was skipped.
The registration details display this under **Waiver signing proof**.

The read confirmation records a scroll or acknowledgement; it does not verify
that the signer understood the document. Signature strokes and photos are
records of the submission, not an identity check.

Photos that were saved are available through the registration's signing
details to users with access to that registration.

### Capture problems

If a photo or drawn signature cannot be kept, the form can save the typed
signature with a warning. Review those warnings and the saved signing details
before relying on the record. A successful entry does not mean that every
requested item was captured.

## Default waivers

Click **Set default** to preselect a waiver on new races. You can set more than
one default, such as your event release and a venue document. Review the
selection on each race before opening registration.

## Versioning and retention

Saved waiver text is not edited in place. Create a new version and attach it
to the relevant races when the wording changes. Existing signature records
retain their recorded version and content hash.

Clearing registrations also deletes associated waiver signatures and guardian
consents. Before clearing real entries, review which records you need to retain
and arrange their retention. The waiver library does not guarantee a retention
period or preserve records that you delete.

Attach waivers from the race's **Waivers** tab. See
