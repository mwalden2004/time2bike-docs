---
title: "Create an organization"
description: "Spin up a new organization to host events."
---

Anyone with a Time2Bike account can create an organization. From the top nav choose **Organizations → Create organization**, or visit [time2.bike/organizations/create](https://time2.bike/organizations/create).

![Create organization form](/images/orgs/create.png)

## What you'll need

| Field | Notes |
|---|---|
| **Name** | Display name shown to riders. |
| **Slug** | Lowercase, hyphens only. Becomes your public URL: `time2.bike/[slug]`. Auto-generated from the name; you can edit it. |
| **Website** | Optional. Linked from your public org page. |
| **Contact email & phone** | Surfaced to riders and on receipts. |
| **Brand color** | Hex value used as the accent on your public pages. |
| **Logo** | Square image, max 5 MB. |

Slugs must match `^[a-z0-9-]+$` and cannot start or end with a hyphen.

## After creation

You land on the **organization dashboard**. From there:

1. Open **Settings → Billing** and connect Stripe — you cannot publish a paid event until this is done.
2. Open **Settings → Team** to invite collaborators.
3. Open **Settings → Waivers** to upload at least one waiver template.
