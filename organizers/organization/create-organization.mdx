Your **organization** is the workspace everything else hangs off: your events,
your races, your money, your crew. You make one, once.

Anyone with a Time2Bike account can create one, and it's free to set up. From
the top navigation choose **Organizations → Create organization**, or go to
[time2.bike/organizations/create](https://time2.bike/organizations/create).

![Create organization form](/images/orgs/create.png)

## What you'll need

| Field | What to put |
|---|---|
| **Name** | What riders should call you. Your club or promotion name. |
| **Web address** | The last part of your public URL: `time2.bike/your-name`. Filled in from your name — edit it if you want something shorter. |
| **Website** | Optional. Linked from your public page. |
| **Contact email & phone** | Riders see these, and they go on receipts. Use an address you'll actually check on race weekend. |
| **Brand color** | The accent color on your public pages. |
| **Logo** | A square image, up to 5 MB. |

<Tip>
  **Pick your web address carefully.** It's the link you'll put on flyers, in
  emails, and on social media. If you change it later, every link anyone
  has already shared stops working. Short and obvious beats clever.
</Tip>

Web addresses can use lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens, and can't start
or end with a hyphen. If the one you want is taken, Time2Bike tells you and
suggests something close.

## What happens next

You land on your **organization dashboard**. It's empty, which is correct. You
haven't done anything yet.

![Dashboard after org creation](/images/orgs/raw.png)

Three things to do before you can take a single entry:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Connect Stripe, before anything else">
    **Settings → Billing**. This is how you get paid, and it's the step with a
    waiting period: Stripe verifies your business and bank details, which can
    take a day or two.

    Start it now even if your first event is months away. You can't publish a
    paid event until it's done.

    → [Stripe payouts](/organizers/organization/settings-billing)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Upload a waiver">
    **Settings → Waivers**. You need at least one before anyone can register.

    → [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers)
  </Step>
  <Step title="Invite your crew">
    **Settings → Team**. Whoever helps you run registration, timing, or the
    money.

    → [Team & invites](/organizers/organization/settings-team)
  </Step>
</Steps>

Then you're ready to build an event.

<Note>
  **Free events don't need Stripe.** If you're just putting on a group ride or
  a free series, skip step one. You only need Stripe once you charge for
  something.
</Note>
