The **Races** tab on an event shows every race (a.k.a. category group) you've created. Each race has its own registration window, pricing, waivers, capacity, and results.

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races → race cards with category counts and capacity bars

## Create a race

1. Click **New race** in the header.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** — e.g. "Cat 1 Men".
   - **Format** — mass start, time trial, downhill, enduro stages, dual slalom bracket, short track, or other. This decides which timing screen your operators get on race day.
   - **Capacity** — max registrants. Leave blank for unlimited.
   - **Categories** — comma-separated category labels (e.g. "Cat 1, Cat 2"). At least one is required.
   - **Gender restriction** — open to everyone, men only, or women only.
   - **Team relay** — toggle on for relay races (lets riders register as a team).
3. **Save**.

## What each card shows

- Race name, format, and whether registration is open, closed, or closed automatically by your cutoff date.
- Categories with current registered counts.
- Capacity fill bar.
- Total revenue from this race.

Click a card to open the **Race overview**.
