The **Races** tab on an event shows every race you've created. A **race** is
what riders register for and what gets timed — it has its own registration
window, pricing, waivers, capacity, and results.

A **group** is only a folder. Use one to keep a long race list readable —
"Cross Country", "Saturday", "Juniors". Races don't need one, and ungrouped
races are listed on their own under **Ungrouped**.

## Create a race

1. Click **New race** — or the **+** on a group to file it there directly.
2. Fill in:
   - **Name** — e.g. "Cat 1 Men".
   - **Format** — mass start, time trial, downhill, enduro stages, dual slalom bracket, short track, or other. This decides which timing screen your operators get on race day.
   - **Group** — optional. Leave it blank and the race lands in **Ungrouped**.
   - **Capacity** — max registrants. Leave blank for unlimited.
   - **Gender restriction** — open to everyone, men only, or women only.
   - **Team relay** — toggle on for relay races (lets riders register as a team).
3. **Save**.

### Pricing and waivers are prefilled

Every race needs a price schedule and at least one waiver, and on a given event
those are near-always the same for every race. Set an organization default once
and the create form fills them in:

- **Default price schedule** — star one at
  [Pricing → Schedules](/organizers/pricing/price-schedules).
- **Default waivers** — star one or more at
  [Settings → Waivers](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers).

A default is a starting point, not a constraint — change either on the race and
your choice wins.

## Groups

- **Add group** — name it. Presets from your organization make common names one tap.
- **Age groups** — set an age-group set on a group and every race in it inherits it. A race can still override its own.
- **Delete group** — the races are **kept** and moved to **Ungrouped**. Deleting a folder never deletes what's in it.

## Search

The search box matches a group's name or any race name inside it, so searching a
group name keeps its whole contents visible.

## What each race shows

- Race name, format, and whether registration is open, closed, or closed automatically by your cutoff date.
- Current registered count.
- Capacity fill bar.
- Total revenue from this race.

Click a race to open the [Race overview](/organizers/races/race-overview).
