title: "Race registrations"
description: "Who's signed up for one race."
---

The race-level **Registrations** tab is a filtered view of just this race's registrations.

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races/[raceId]/registrations → registrations table

Columns:

- **Athlete name** and **email**.
- **Status** — paid / pending / cancelled / refunded.
- **Bib** — current plate (see [Bibs](/organizers/events/bibs)).
- **Paid** — amount paid (after discounts, before service fee).
- **Registered** — timestamp.

Use this when you want a clean cut of one race only. For full editing, manual entry, refunds, and CSV export of the whole event, use [Event registrations](/organizers/registrations/event-registrations).
