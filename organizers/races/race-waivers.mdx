Open **Race → Waivers** to attach waivers from your org's [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers) to this specific race.

## Assign

Check the box next to each waiver to require it on this race. Click **Save**.

If your event uses separate adult and minor documents, attach the appropriate
versions and review their **Applies to** settings. Document selection uses the
rider's birthday and the platform's configured age threshold; it does not
establish a document's legal effect.

<Note>
  That filtering is applied wherever Time2Bike collects a signature on a rider's
  behalf — the [check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) and
  [manual entry](/organizers/registrations/manual-entry). Riders registering
  through checkout are still shown every waiver attached to the race.

  → [Who signs](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers#who-signs-riders-and-guardians)
</Note>

## Removing a waiver

Unchecking a waiver removes it from the race's required documents. It does not
delete signatures already recorded. Clearing the registrations themselves does
delete associated signatures; review retention needs before clearing entries.
