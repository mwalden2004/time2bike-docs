title: "Race waivers"
description: "Pick which waivers riders must sign for this race."
---

Open **Race → Waivers** to attach waivers from your org's [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers) to this specific race.

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races/[raceId]/waivers → waiver list with checkboxes and "N assigned" count

## Assign

Check the box next to each waiver to require it on this race. Click **Save**.

A waiver set to **Adults** isn't shown to riders under 18, and a **Minors** waiver isn't shown to adults. If you need both covered, attach one of each — Time2Bike shows each rider the right one based on their birthday.

## Removing a waiver

Unchecking a waiver removes the requirement for **future** registrations. Existing signatures stay on the registrations that have already been submitted — your audit trail is intact.
