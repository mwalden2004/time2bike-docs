Open **Race → Waivers** to attach waivers from your org's [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers) to this specific race.

[IMAGE: /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races/[raceId]/waivers → waiver list with checkboxes and "N assigned" count]

## Assign

Check the box next to each waiver to require it on this race. Click **Save**.

If `appliesTo` on the waiver is `adult`, minors who register won't see it (and vice versa). Use one `adult` waiver and one `minor` waiver if you need both populations covered.

## Removing a waiver

Unchecking a waiver removes the requirement for **future** registrations. Existing signatures stay on the registrations that have already been submitted — your audit trail is intact.

[IMAGE: /events/[slug]/register → Waivers step showing two required waivers]
