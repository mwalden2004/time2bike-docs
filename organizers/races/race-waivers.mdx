Open **Race → Waivers** to attach waivers from your org's [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers) to this specific race.

{/* IMAGE NEEDED: /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races/[raceId]/waivers → waiver list with checkboxes and "N assigned" count */}

## Assign

Check the box next to each waiver to require it on this race. Click **Save**.

If you need adults and minors covered by different documents, attach one of
each. A waiver's **Applies to** setting decides which riders get which, based on
their birthday — and the threshold is the age of majority in the state the
waiver is governed in, which is 19 in Alabama and Nebraska and 21 in Mississippi
and Puerto Rico rather than a flat 18.

<Note>
  That filtering is applied wherever Time2Bike collects a signature on a rider's
  behalf — the [check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) and
  [manual entry](/organizers/registrations/manual-entry). Riders registering
  through checkout are still shown every waiver attached to the race.

  → [Who signs](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers#who-signs-riders-and-guardians)
</Note>

## Removing a waiver

Unchecking a waiver removes the requirement for **future** registrations. Existing signatures stay on the registrations that have already been submitted — your audit trail is intact.
