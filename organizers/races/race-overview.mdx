Clicking a race opens the **Race overview** at `/organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races/[raceId]`. The Race nav has tabs for **Overview**, **Registrations**, **Pricing**, **Waivers**, and **Results**.

> !!-IMAGE-NEEDED-!! — /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/races/[raceId] → race overview with registration count and quick edit form

## Editable fields

- **Name** and **format**.
- **Group** — which folder this race is filed under on the races list, or none. See [Groups & races](/organizers/races/races-list).
- **Capacity** — total seats.
- **Gender restriction** — who may enter: open to everyone, men only, or women only.
- **Score men and women separately** — see [Splitting one race into several results](#splitting-one-race-into-several-results).
- **Team relay** — toggle.
- **Registration disabled** — manual kill switch for this race.
- **Cutoff days** — close registration N days before start.
- **Explicit close** — exact close date/time.
- **FAQ** — race-specific Q&A rendered on the public race page.
- **Info links** — extra resources (rule book, course map).
- **Contact** — name / email / phone shown on the public race page.

## Splitting one race into several results

Two toggles let one race on the ground produce more than one result, so you
don't have to build separate races and keep two sets of pricing, waivers, and
timing config in step.

- **Age-group split** — score each age bracket separately. Brackets come from
  the age-group set on the race or its group.
- **Score men and women separately** — score each gender separately.

Everybody registers for and starts the **same** race. Placement, gaps, and
exports are computed per group, so each one gets its own leader and its own
POS 1. Turn both on and you get a result per age bracket per gender.

<Note>
  **Score men and women separately** is independent of **Gender restriction**.
  One decides how results are scored; the other decides who may enter at all. A
  race that's already restricted to one gender has nothing to split.
</Note>

Riders with no gender on file are grouped under **Unspecified** rather than
being folded into the men's or women's result. Unlike age — which drifts with
the calendar and is frozen at registration — gender is read live from the
rider's profile.

The split carries through to [results](/organizers/races/race-results), live
results, and the [printable results PDF](/timing/results/exports), which renders
one section per group.

## Recent registrations panel

The right rail shows the last few registrants in this race with their bib, paid amount, and status. Click any row to open the registration editor.

## Save & discard

Any edit shows the **Save** button. Navigating away with unsaved changes prompts a confirm modal.
