Each race has its own price schedule. Pick from your org's templates (see [Price schedules](/organizers/pricing/price-schedules)) under **Race → Pricing**.

## Apply a template

1. Open **Pricing** under the race.
2. Select a card under **Choose a template**.
3. Click **Apply template**.

The race uses the selected template. Editing that template updates prices for future registrations on every race that uses it. Existing registrations keep the price they were charged.

## Tier model

The **Currently applied** section shows the schedule's mode and price tiers.
The preview lists each tier's name, when it applies, any cap, and its price.

Templates support **Flat rate**, **Days before event**, and **By registration
count** pricing. See [Price schedules](/organizers/pricing/price-schedules) to
configure a template.

## Free races

To make a race free, apply a template with a single $0 tier, or set the price to 0 on the schedule. Free races still go through the cart and confirmation flow; Stripe isn't charged.
