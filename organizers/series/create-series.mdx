A **series** is a multi-event championship. Series can share plates, run a combined standings page, and let you sell a series-wide discount code.

Create one from **Series → New series** in your organization.

</Frame>

## Fields

- **Series Name** — display name.
- **URL Slug (Optional)** — the last part of the series' public link. → [Web addresses](/platform/slugs)
- **Description (Optional)** — short blurb for the public series page.
- **Start Date / End Date** — series window.
- **Link Events (Optional)** — check the boxes for every event that belongs in the series.

## After create

You return to the series list. Open the new series to:

- Add or remove events.
- Open the **Edit** page to change the name, web address, or dates.
- Open **Plates** to assign series-scoped plate numbers.
- Click **Public page** to see what riders see.
