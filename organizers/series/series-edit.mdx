---
title: "Edit a series"
description: "Rename, re-slug, or reschedule a series."
---

The **Edit** tab under a series exposes:

- **Name**
- **Slug** — changing it breaks old `time2.bike/series/[old-slug]` links unless you redirect.
- **Description**
- **Start date** / **end date**

[IMAGE: /organizations/[id]/series/[seriesId]/edit → edit form]

Click **Save** to apply. The change is immediate; rider-facing pages refresh on next load.

To change which events are in the series, use the **Overview** tab instead.
