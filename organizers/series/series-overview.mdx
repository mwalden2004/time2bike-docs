The series **Overview** is the home screen for one series.

## Header

Series name, web address, date range, and a **View public page** button. The
public page shows the combined event calendar plus the series standings.

## Events in this series

A table of every event in the series with name, dates, status, and a **remove** button. Click **Add event** to attach an existing event from your org.

Each row links to the event overview.

## Sub-tabs

- **Edit** — change the name, web address, description, or dates (see [Edit a series](/organizers/series/series-edit)).
- **Plates** — assign series-scoped plate numbers used as fallback bibs across every race in the series (see [Series plates](/organizers/series/series-plates)).

## Standings

**Series standings** appears on the public series page. Publish individual race
results with linked rider profiles to start the table. Riders choose a **Field**,
search for their name, and see their total alongside each round's points and
placing. Each round links to its event results.

Only **published** events contribute. Draft, unlisted, hidden, archived, deleted
and taken-down events stay out, as do events belonging to suspended or deleted
organizations. Relay team results do not contribute to individual standings.

### How rounds match

Keep the race name and its group name consistent across events. Standings match
those names without regard to capitalization or extra spaces, along with the
discipline, race format, gender restriction and scoring splits. When your events
already use linked league divisions, those links identify the field instead of
the names.

Age groups and genders get separate standings when you score them separately.
Use the same age-group set across the series. If an event has two races with
the same matching details, those races stay separate across the series rather
than sharing a season total. Give distinct fields distinct names.

Each rider's results combine through their linked profile. A result entered
with only a name still affects that race's placing, but cannot contribute to a
rider's season total. The table shows how many results need a profile link.

### Points and ties

Standings use **Time2Bike points**, with this fixed scale:

| Place | Points |
| --- | --- |
| 1–5 | 100, 90, 82, 76, 72 |
| 6–10 | 69, 67, 65, 63, 61 |
| 11–20 | 60 down to 51, one fewer per place |
| 21 onward | One fewer per place, with a minimum of 30 |

Every race counts. There are no dropped rounds. Equal season totals share a
place; the next place skips the tied riders, such as 1, 1, 3. This table uses
Time2Bike's rules; your league's championship may score differently.

Published placings take precedence. When a race has no recorded placings,
finishers are placed by completed laps and finish time; identical results share
a place. If only some finishers have published placings, the others need a
placing before earning points. Unplaced bracket runs stay unplaced.

DNF, DNS, disqualified, recused and unplaced results earn zero. A rider with no
published result for a round has a dash in that column and earns no points.

### Corrections and publication

Both **provisional** and **official** results count. Provisional scores carry an
asterisk, and their field says **Includes provisional results**. Results must
have one of those publication states; draft results stay out.

Correct or withdraw a result from your event's results tools. Standings rebuild
when loaded, and the public page refreshes them every minute while open. There
is no separate standings publish button.
