The **Landing** tab controls everything riders see on your event page.

![Landing page editor](/images/orgs/landing.png)

The editor has three sub-tabs.

## Details

- **Description** — markdown body of the hero.
- **Web address** — the `spring-classic` in `time2.bike/events/spring-classic`. → [Web addresses](/platform/slugs)
- **Refund policy** — appears in the rider's cart and on the rider event page.
- **FAQ** — questions about the event as a whole: parking, packet pickup, the weather plan. See below.

## FAQ

Add question-and-answer rows for the things riders email you about. They render
as one **FAQ** section on the public event page.

Questions about a *single* race — a start-time quirk, a category rule — belong
on that race instead, under its **FAQ** field
([Race overview](/organizers/races/race-overview)). Those appear in the same
section on the public page, attributed to their race, so a rider finds
everything in one place.

<Note>
  Rows with a question but no answer are dropped when you save — otherwise
  riders would click one open and find nothing inside.
</Note>

FAQ entries are deliberately not drawn inside the race rows: those rows are the
registration click target, and an expandable question inside a selectable row is
a control inside a control.

## Images

- **Banner image** — the big image at the top. You'll be asked for a short
  description of the photo, which is what screen readers read out.
- **Gallery** — extra photos further down the page, each with its own
  description.

→ [Images](/platform/uploads-and-cdn)

## Maps

For each race, paste the share link from a map — a Strava or RideWithGPS route,
for example. Riders see one map per race in the **Course** section of your
event page.

## Preview & publish

**Preview** shows you the rider's view of the page including edits you haven't
saved yet. **Save** keeps them. Riders see saved changes straight away, as long
as the event is **published**.
