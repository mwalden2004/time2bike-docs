**Event → Sponsors** controls the logos on this event's public page. Two
separate things live here, and it helps to keep them apart:

- **This event's sponsors** — logos that belong to this event alone. You create,
  edit, and delete them here.
- **Organization sponsors** — your usual logos, inherited from
  [Settings → Sponsors](/organizers/organization/settings-sponsors). This screen
  decides how many of them this event picks up.

## Add a sponsor to this event

1. Click **Add sponsor**.
2. Fill in **name**, **website**, and upload a **logo**.
3. **Save**.

Sponsors created here are always shown on this event and appear nowhere else.
Use them for a one-off title sponsor or a venue partner who isn't part of your
regular roster.

## Choose what you inherit

An event isn't always your organization's own shop window. A club timing
somebody else's race, or a one-off with its own title sponsor, may want few of
its usual logos or none at all. Pick one of:

| Option | What the event shows |
|---|---|
| **All** | Every org sponsor that hasn't opted out of events. This is the default, and what every event did before this setting existed. |
| **Selected** | Only the org sponsors you tick. |
| **None** | No org sponsors at all. |

The list shows every org sponsor with its current state, so you can see at a
glance what this event is carrying.

<Note>
  A sponsor you've set to **Org only** in organization settings still appears in
  this list. Ticking it here is a deliberate override — it's how you put a
  normally page-only sponsor on one specific event.
</Note>

Changing the policy affects **this event only**. It never edits the sponsors
themselves and never touches another event.

## League sponsors

If your event is a round of a league, the league's own sponsors always appear
and can't be dropped from here. They're contractual across the season and
aren't the host organization's to remove.

## Who can change this

Organization **admins** and **managers**. Everyone else sees the screen
read-only.

## Where riders see them

Sponsors render in the sponsor grid on the public event page, each linked to its
site. Riders see them in one grid regardless of which of the three sources they
came from.
