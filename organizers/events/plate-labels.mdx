**Event → Plates → Assign & print** is the race-morning desk. Use it when you own
the plates: they arrive in a box, pre-printed, and each rider gets whichever one
is next.

The Plates table is for sorting a whole field out in advance. This page is for
the twenty minutes when there is a queue.

## Set the label up first

The first time you open the page, you choose what the sticker looks like. The
preview on the right is rendered by the same code that drives your printer — if
a name is clipped there, it will be clipped on the sticker.

**Label stock** covers the common thermal rolls and three sheet layouts:

- **Thermal roll** — one label per page, at the exact size of the label you have
  loaded. Your printer has nothing to scale.
- **Sheet** — Avery 5160, 5163 and 5164, laid out on US Letter for a laser or
  inkjet printer.
- **Custom size** — anything between 0.75 and 8.5 inches on a side.

**What goes on the label** is yours to pick. The plate number prints large on the
left; everything else stacks beside it:

| Field | Notes |
| --- | --- |
| Plate number | Printed large. This is the part read from a distance. |
| Rider name | |
| Race | A rider on one plate across several races reads as "Cat 3 Men +2 more". |
| Category | Only when the race sits under one. |
| Team / club | Their registered team, or the club they entered under. |
| Age group | The bracket they entered in. |
| Age | Whole years on your event's age-on date. |
| Emergency contact | Name and phone from the rider's profile. |
| Chip / transponder | Only when one is paired to the plate. |
| Event name and date | Worth printing when you reuse plate stock across a season. |

**Print a hairline round each label** draws an outline you can hold against a
sheet to check alignment before committing a box of Avery to it.

Your choices are remembered for this event, so you don't re-pick them at 7am.
Change them later from **Label setup** in the header.

<Note>
A label cannot grow. Ask for more than fits on 2.25" stock and text shrinks,
then truncates — but never the emergency phone number, and the contact's
relationship goes before their name does. The preview shows you exactly what
survives.
</Note>

## Assign at the desk

**Next plate off the stack** is the number you are about to hand over. Find the
rider, and give it to them:

- Search for a name and press **Enter** to give them the number.
- Or click **Give 142** on their row.

The number then moves on by itself, skipping anything already assigned.

**Skip** walks to the next free number — use it when the plate you expected isn't
in the box. If you are holding something else entirely, type it into the number
field instead. Letters are allowed (`A12`), though there is no "next" after one,
so type each in by hand.

**Plate covers** decides how far the number reaches:

- **The whole event** — one plate per rider, whatever they entered. What most
  events want.
- **One race** — a rider in two races gets two numbers.
- **One category** — one plate shared across the races inside a category.

If the number is already taken, the assignment is refused and you're told who has
it. That check knows about season-long series plates, not just the ones assigned
here.

## Printing as you go, or all at once

**Print the label as I assign** is on by default: one rider, one sticker, handed
over with the plate. Nothing is downloaded — the label goes straight to your
printer.

Turn it off to run a fast assignment desk and print in one batch afterwards.
Either way, **Print all labels** prints every plate assigned so far. Riders
without a number are skipped, so you can run it again later for the ones who
turned up late.

In that dialog you can:

- Limit it to particular **races**.
- Set the **order** — plate number by default, which means a printed batch lays
  straight onto a stack of plates in the same order.
- Tell it how many **labels are already used** on a part-used Avery sheet, so it
  starts partway down the page.
- **Save PDF** instead of printing, if you'd rather send the file somewhere else.

To reprint one, use **Reprint** in the **Just assigned** list, or **Label** on any
assigned rider's row.

<Note>
If your browser opens the label in a new tab instead of printing it, that's the
browser refusing to drive its own print dialog for a document it didn't navigate
to. Print from that tab. Chrome and Edge print directly.
</Note>

## Emergency contacts

The **Emergency contact** field is the usual reason a label goes on the *back* of
a plate — a medic can read it when the rider can't answer.

It comes from the rider's profile. Riders who never filled one in simply have no
line for it; the label closes up round the gap rather than printing an empty
heading.
