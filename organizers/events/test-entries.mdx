Before you announce an event, you can register for it yourself and see exactly
what a rider sees: the real prices, the real waivers, the real confirmation
email. Entries placed while the event is a **draft** are **test entries**: they
take no money and are flagged so you can delete them in one action later.

## Try the entry flow

<Steps>
  <Step title="Leave the event in Draft">
    Only your organization can open a draft event's page. Everyone else gets a
    404, so there's no risk of a rider stumbling into your dry run.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register as you normally would">
    Open your own event page, pick races, and go through checkout. You'll
    see your real price schedule, waivers, custom questions, and age groups.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check out for free">
    Test entries skip Stripe entirely and settle as free, whatever the price
    schedule says. Nothing is charged and no payout is affected.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Read the confirmation email">
    It's the same email a rider gets, so it's a good last check on your event
    name, times, and location.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only people in your organization can place test entries: the owner, org
  members, and Time2Bike staff. A signed-out visitor still gets a 404 on a
  draft event.
</Note>

## How test entries behave

- They're marked **Test** in the registrations table, so they're never mistaken
  for a real rider.
- They're excluded from revenue reporting.
- The flag sticks after you publish. Publishing does **not** convert them into
  real entries, and it does not delete them.

<Warning>
  Test entries appear on the public start list once the event leaves draft.
  Clear them before you publish.
</Warning>

Event **Settings** warns you about this: if you switch away from **Draft** while
test entries are still on the event, it tells you how many there are and links
you straight to the tool below.

## Clear entries

Open **Event → Registrations** and click **Clear entries**. Time2Bike counts
what's there first and shows you the numbers before anything is deleted.

| Scope | What it removes | Who can run it |
|---|---|---|
| **Test entries only** | Just the dry runs you placed against the draft. No real rider is touched. | Admin or manager |
| **Everything** | Every entry on the event, imports and paid entries included. | Organization owner |

**Everything** asks you to type the event's name back before it will run.

<Warning>
  Clearing entries is permanent and it does **not** refund anybody. Deleting a
  paid entry leaves that rider charged with nothing to show for it. If you want
  people's money back, use **Refund all** first. See
  [Editing and refunds](/organizers/registrations/editing-and-refunds).
</Warning>

## What gets cleared alongside

A registration doesn't sit on its own, so clearing one also removes what was
created with it:

- Waiver signatures and guardian consents
- Discount redemptions. Each affected code's usage count is recounted, so a
  cleared test entry gives the use back
- League memberships earned by the entry
- Start-list entries, once the rider has no other entry left in that race
- Relay teams, once no remaining entry still points at them

**Plate assignments are kept.** They're records you maintain, and series and
league-season plates outlive any single event.

An event that Time2Bike has frozen or taken down can't be cleared.

## Going live

1. Clear the test entries.
2. Set the status to **Published**, or **Unlisted** if you want a link-only
   soft launch. See [Event settings](/organizers/events/event-settings).
