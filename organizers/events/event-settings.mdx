## Status

Status is a set of distinct visibilities, not a progression. Pick the one that
matches who you want to reach.

| Status | Who can open it | Registration |
|---|---|---|
| **Draft** | Your organization only. Everyone else gets a 404. | Open to your own staff, and every entry is a free [test entry](/organizers/events/test-entries). |
| **Unlisted** | Anyone with the link. | Real, at the real price. |
| **Published** | Everyone. Listed publicly. | Real, controlled by each race's window. |
| **Archived** | Everyone. | Closed. Results stay readable. |
| **Hidden** | Your organization only. | Closed. |

Changing status is instant.

### Draft

A draft is your staging area. Your organization can open the page and walk the
whole entry flow — real prices, real waivers, real confirmation email — without
taking money. Those entries are flagged and should be cleared before you go
live. See [Test entries](/organizers/events/test-entries).

If you switch away from **Draft** with test entries still on the event, this
screen tells you how many and links you to the tool that clears them.

### Unlisted vs hidden

These two get confused, and they're opposites:

- **Unlisted** is *shareable but not advertised*. The link works for anyone,
  registration is real and takes payment, but the event stays out of the public
  events list, search results, and your sitemap. Use it for soft launches and
  invite-only events.
- **Hidden** is *internal only*. The event is off every public surface and
  can't be reached even by a direct link. Team practices and staff takedowns
  land here.

### Archived

Registration is closed and the event drops out of upcoming-event lists, but
results stay published and readable. This is where an event belongs once it's
over.

## Permitting & sanctioning

If your event is sanctioned, set the **Permit issuer** and (optionally) **Permit ID** here. Supported issuers: `USAC`, `UCI`, `T2B`, or a `CUSTOM` issuer name you provide.

Setting a permit issuer:

- Adds a **Sanctioned** badge to the public event page.
- Enables the **License** check on the registration **Review & pay** step — see [Rider licenses](/riders/registering/licenses).
- Enables the **Licenses** tab in the event admin for tracking one-day filings — see [Licenses](/organizers/events/licenses).

UCI-permitted events automatically disable one-day licenses (UCI requires an annual license).

## Collaborating organizations

You can give another organization editing access to this event by entering
their **web address** — the `socalmtb` in `time2.bike/socalmtb`. They appear in
the list below; click the **×** to take it away again.

Collaborators can edit everything except settings/billing-level fields. Useful for:

- A series host running an event the host org will physically run.
- Two clubs co-presenting a race.
- A timing company managing race-day data on your behalf.

Only the owning org (the one that created the event) shows in the **Event owner** label; collaborators see the event in their own dashboard alongside their owned events.
