**Event → Event setup → Licenses** groups riders using the license and payment
information recorded for the event. Use it to review annual licenses on file,
reconcile one-day fees, and prepare filings for your sanctioning body.

This page only appears on events that have a **permit issuer** configured. Set that on **Event → Settings** under **Permitting & Sanctioning**, or on the create-event wizard.

## Default view: one-day licenses

The default filter is **One-day**. Use these rows to reconcile one-day fees and prepare the filing your issuer requires. For each row you'll see:

| Column | What it is |
|---|---|
| **Participant** | The racer's name. |
| **Race** | The specific race they entered. |
| **Issuer** | The sanctioning body code (`USAC`, `UCI`, `CUSTOM`, etc.). |
| **Tier** | `beginner` or `experienced`. |
| **Fee** | What the rider paid, or what a one-day costs here if nobody has charged them. |
| **Coverage** | What you have to do about this rider — see below. |
| **Submission status** | Your filing status — see below. |

## Coverage

| Badge | What it means |
|---|---|
| **Annual on file** | An annual license recorded as covering race day. Check the issuer's event requirements. |
| **One-day** | A one-day fee you collected at checkout. File it. |
| **One-day — paid at source** | The imported file records a one-day purchase. Reconcile it before collecting another fee or filing. |
| **One-day — not collected** | Nobody has taken the fee. Collect it at the desk, then file. |
| **Licence required** | No qualifying license is recorded, and one-day purchase is unavailable. Resolve eligibility under the issuer's rules before the rider starts. |

Rows that aren't covered show why underneath — *Licence expired*, *No licence
on file* — so you can tell a renewal from a first-timer at a glance.

Riders brought in by an [import](/organizers/events/imports#licence-check)
appear here like anyone else. Before, this page only listed entries that went
through checkout, so an imported field was invisible on it entirely.

## Licences settled at the desk

The [check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) and
[manual entry](/organizers/registrations/manual-entry) can both settle a licence
on race morning, and whatever they do lands here:

- **A licence number keyed in** is saved to the rider's profile after the
  available checks. USA Cycling numbers use an online lookup; other issuers
  currently have no live verification. Review the license's dates and the
  issuer's requirements before treating the rider as eligible.
- **A one-day sold** — cash, card, or waived — becomes a one-day row to file,
  tagged with how it was collected.

A **waived** one-day records a fee of zero and remains in the filing list.
Waiving the charge in Time2Bike does not establish that the issuer has issued
a license or waived any amount you owe. Reconcile it under the issuer's rules.

One fee per rider per race date, however many races they entered — the same rule
the cart applies — so a rider in three races produces one row to file, not
three.

## Filing status

Each row has a four-state status you cycle as you process the file:

- **Not started** — not yet filed.
- **In progress** — you're working on it.
- **Submitted** — filed with the sanctioning body. You can add a **reference number** for the filing.
- **N/A** — exclude this row (e.g. you've reconciled it elsewhere).

Click the status pill on a row to advance it, or open the **notes** modal to add notes and a reference number.

## Annual-license view

Toggle the filter to **Annual** to review licenses on file. This view reflects
the stored number, status, and dates. It does not confirm every event-specific
eligibility requirement or replace checks required by your sanctioning body.

## Group by participant

Switch **Group by** to **Participant** to roll up riders who entered multiple races. Useful when one person has three days' worth of one-day fees and you only need to file once.

## Export

**Download CSV** exports the current filtered view with all columns — including
who took the fee and the license's status. Check the issuer's required format
before uploading it.
