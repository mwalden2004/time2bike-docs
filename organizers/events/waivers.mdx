Waivers are authored at the **organization** level (see [Waiver library](/organizers/organization/settings-waivers)) and attached at the **race** level.

The Event-level Waivers screen lets you bulk-assign waivers across all races in the event in one place. For per-race control, use the race's **Waivers** tab.

[IMAGE: /organizations/[id]/events/[eventId]/waivers → grid of waivers with race-assignment toggles]

## How it works

1. Open **Event → Waivers**.
2. For each waiver in your library, toggle which races require it.
3. **Save**.

Adult-only waivers won't be enforced for minor registrants and vice versa — `appliesTo` on the waiver decides.

## What riders see

Each required waiver appears as its own step during registration with a typed-initial signature. Time2Bike records the version, content hash, IP, user agent, and timestamp.

See [Waivers (rider view)](/riders/registering/waivers) for the rider-side experience.
