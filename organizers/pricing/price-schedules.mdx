A **price schedule** is a named, reusable set of tiers you can apply to any race. Manage them at **Pricing → Schedules**.

## Tier model

Choose a pricing mode:

| Mode | Purpose |
|---|---|
| **Flat** | One fixed entry price. |
| **Days before** | Price tiers based on the days remaining before the race. |
| **By signups** | Price tiers based on the race's paid registration count. |

Each price or tier has a **Name** and **Price**. Date-based tiers also have
**Days before event**; signup-based tiers have **After this many registrations**.

## Create a schedule

1. Click **New schedule**.
2. Set the **name**, **currency**, and **pricing mode**. Add tiers with **Add tier**, or use **Set price** for a flat schedule.
3. **Save**.

## Set a default

Click **Set default** on a schedule to preselect it on every new race you
create. Exactly one schedule can be the default.

The create form preselects your default. Review it for each race and choose a
different schedule where needed.

→ [Groups & races](/organizers/races/races-list)

## Apply to a race

Open the race's **Pricing** tab (see [Race pricing](/organizers/races/race-pricing)). The race uses the schedule's current prices. Later template edits affect future registrations on races that use it; existing registrations keep the price they were charged.

## Edit, clone, delete

From the schedule list:

- **Edit** — update the schedule and its price tiers.
- **Clone** — duplicate as a starting point.
- **Delete** — remove a schedule after reviewing the confirmation.
