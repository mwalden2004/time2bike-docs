## Issue a refund

You can refund a specific registration from this page too:

1. Click **Process refund**.
2. Enter the **registration ID** (or open a registration from the [Event registrations](/organizers/registrations/event-registrations) page and refund from there — easier).
3. Enter the **amount** (USD) and optional **reason**.
4. Click **Process refund**.

<Frame caption="Example screen with fictional demo data.">
  <img src="/images/organizers/refund-modal.png" alt="Process refund dialog with an example registration ID, amount, and reason" />
</Frame>

Time2Bike submits the refund to Stripe for the original payment method. The
amount cannot exceed the registration's remaining refundable balance. The
registration changes to `refunded` when recorded refunds cover that refundable
portion; the status does not confirm bank receipt.

The registration refund excludes the platform service fee. See
[Editing and refunds](/organizers/registrations/editing-and-refunds) for what is
refundable and how to handle platform-error or fraud claims.

If the result is unclear or the request times out, check Stripe before trying
again. A refund can be pending or fail, and a confirmation in Time2Bike does
not establish that the rider's bank has credited it.

## What's logged

Each refund row stores:

- **Registration** — the registration identifier.
- **Amount** refunded (in dollars).
- **Reason** (your note).
- **Processor** — the payment processor.
- **Refunded** — the refund date and time.

## Bulk view

Compare these records with your Stripe statement, including refunds started
directly in Stripe. Investigate missing, pending, or failed refunds before
treating the totals as reconciled.
