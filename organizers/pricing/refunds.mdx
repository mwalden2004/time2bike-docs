## Issue a refund

You can refund a specific registration from this page too:

1. Click **Issue refund**.
2. Enter the **registration ID** (or open a registration from the [Event registrations](/organizers/registrations/event-registrations) page and refund from there — easier).
3. Enter the **amount** (USD) and optional **reason**.
4. Submit.

Time2Bike issues the refund through Stripe on the original payment method. The registration flips to `refunded`.

## What's logged

Each refund row stores:

- Registration link, event, race.
- **Amount** refunded (in dollars).
- **Reason** (your note).
- **Issued by** — the org member who initiated it.
- **Stripe refund ID** for cross-referencing in your Stripe dashboard.

## Bulk view

Use this page to reconcile your monthly Stripe statement against Time2Bike-issued refunds — every refund here corresponds to a Stripe refund on your connected account.
