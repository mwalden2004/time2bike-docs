Being on a team doesn't change how you enter a race. It changes what shows up
next to your name in the results.

## The rider picks their team at registration

This is the part people expect to be more complicated than it is. **The team
shown next to a rider's name is chosen by that rider during registration, on
every entry.** Use the affiliation you are entitled to represent under the
event's rules.

That means it can be:

- **A Time2Bike club** you're a member of.
- **A college or high-school program**.
- **A shop, a sponsor, or whoever pays your entries**.
- **Nothing** — a personal entry.

Riders on a Time2Bike team get theirs filled in automatically from their
profile's default. Everyone else types a name in a free-text field. Both end up
in the same place: next to the rider's name in the start list and the results.

<Note>
  Nothing about the free-text version requires a team to exist on Time2Bike. A
  rider can type "Cascade Cycling" whether or not there's a Cascade Cycling
  club here. Being on a real team just saves them the typing and unlocks
  everything else in this section: rosters, practices, bulk registration.
</Note>

## Racing under your team

Riders pick their team during registration, on the participant row for each
race: **Racing for Coldwater Composite**.

The easiest way to make that automatic is a **default team**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the rider's profile">
    From [time2.bike/profiles](https://time2.bike/profiles), click the rider you
    want to set a default team on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Find the Race Team card">
    It only appears if that rider belongs to at least one team.

    <Frame caption="A rider profile’s Race Team card with a default team selected. Demo data.">
      <img src="/images/teams/race-team.png" alt="A rider profile’s Race Team card with a default team selected." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick the default">
    Choose a team, or **Personal entry (no team shown)**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

From then on, that team is applied automatically every time they register, and
it's shown next to their name in results. You can still remove it for a one-off
personal entry during any single registration.

### Riders not on a Time2Bike team

Anyone can type a free-text team, club, or sponsor name during registration,
using the **+ Add team / club / sponsor name** link on the participant row. It's
stored on that one registration and shown in results. Sponsored riders use it to
credit whoever pays their entry.

## Registering several members at once

Team owners and admins can put multiple riders into one race and pay in a single
checkout.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get permission from riders or guardians">
    Confirm that each rider wants the entry and who is authorized to sign their
    waivers. The roster's **Consent** setting does not prevent owners or admins
    from using bulk registration. See [Roster & roles](/teams/roster).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the team and click Bulk register team">
    It's on the team **Overview**, under *Race together*.

    <Frame caption="Bulk registration with Members, Event, and Race columns. Demo data.">
      <img src="/images/teams/bulk-register.png" alt="Bulk registration with Members, Event, and Race columns." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Pick members, an event, and a race">
    Three columns, left to right. Search the event list if it's long.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Continue to checkout">
    You land in the normal [registration flow](/riders/registering/registration-flow)
    with everyone pre-filled and the team already attached. Review per-member
    pricing and waivers, then pay once.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Relay races aren't available for bulk registration. Only individual-format
  races are listed. Relay teams are built inside the registration flow itself
  and are a different thing from your club team.
</Note>

## What organizers see

Organizers running the event see each rider's team affiliation alongside their
name in registrations, start lists, and results. A role in the organizer's
workspace does not by itself grant team-management access, or vice versa.

See [Teams, from an organizer's side](/organizers/organization/teams).
