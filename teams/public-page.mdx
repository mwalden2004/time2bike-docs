Public and unlisted teams have a page such as `time2.bike/team/bay-area-cc`.
It's what people find
when they're looking for a club to join, and it's where the **Apply to join**
button lives.

  <img src="/images/teams/public-page.png" alt="A team public page with a banner, logo, About section, and roster." />
</Frame>

Admins edit it from the **Public Page** tab.

## What's on it

| Section | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| **Banner** | Settings → Branding |
| **Logo** | Settings → Branding |
| **Team name and short bio** | Settings → Basic info |
| **Location and website** | Settings |
| **Member count** | Your roster |
| **Apply to join** | Appears when join policy is **Application required** |
| **About** | Public Page → About |
| **Roster** | Public Page → Show roster |

## Editing it

The **Public Page** tab controls:

- **About** — a longer description than the short bio. Line breaks are kept.
- **Header image** — the image at the top of the page.
- **Show roster** — list your members publicly. On by default.
- **Show results history** — off by default.

Branding, meaning the logo and banner, is set under [Settings](/teams/settings),
because it's used in more places than just this page.

## Visibility

Your team's **visibility** setting decides who can reach the page at all:

| Visibility | Effect |
|---|---|
| **Public** | Listed and discoverable |
| **Unlisted** | Reachable by link, but not listed |
| **Private** | Public page unavailable; members use the team workspace |

Set it under [Settings → Visibility & joining](/teams/settings).

Anyone with an unlisted page's link can open and share it. Before enabling
**Show roster**, review which member names and roles will be visible, including
any minors on the team.

## Your web address

The last part of the address, the `bay-area-cc` bit, is set when you create
the team. Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only, at least two
characters, and it can't start or end with a hyphen.

Teams, organizations, events, and series all share one pool of names, so the
one you want may already be taken. The form tells you and suggests something
close. See [Web addresses](/platform/slugs).
