The **Results** tab is the coach's clipboard. It replaces the paper version: one
grid, athletes down the side, practices across the top.

Available to **coaches**, **managers**, team **admins**, and **owners**.

{/* IMAGE NEEDED: The **Results** clipboard: a week expanded, athletes in rows, activity columns, and the trend column on the right */}

## Reading the grid

- **Rows** are your roster athletes.
- **Columns** are activities — one per activity per practice, labelled with the
  activity name and the date.
- **Cells** are that athlete's time in that activity. A blank cell means they
  weren't timed, which is usually the attendance answer you were after.
- **Trend** on the right shows whether an athlete is getting faster or slower.

Practices are grouped by week, newest first, and each week collapses. (Practices
group by *month* on the Practices tab and by *week* here — comparing times is a
different job from finding a date.)

## Controls

| Control | What it does |
|---|---|
| **Group by gender** | Splits the rows into groups so you're comparing like with like when setting targets |
| **Show splits** | Adds a column per split alongside each total, when the activity recorded them |
| **Season filter** | Narrows to one season |
| **Export** | Downloads the grid |
| **New season** | Creates a season you can tag practices to |

There's a filter bar across the top for narrowing the athletes and activities in
view.

## Trends and targets

A median row gives you the group's time for each activity, which is what you
actually compare an athlete against when setting a target for the next session.
With **Group by gender** on, the comparison is within each group.

Click an athlete to open their history — every practice they've been timed at,
so you can see the shape of their season rather than one bad Tuesday.

## Where the times come from

Practice times come from live timing, not from published results — practices are
never published, so the live-timing rows are the only source. This means:

- Times appear as soon as they're recorded at the timer.
- You don't have to finalize or publish anything for the clipboard to fill in.
- Nothing here is public, ever.

## Seasons

Click **New season**, give it a name and a start date (and an optional end
date), then tag practices to it from the Practices tab. The clipboard and the
practice list both filter by season.

<Tip>
  Create the season *before* your first practice of the year. Tagging practices
  as you go is a two-second job; tagging forty of them in April is not.
</Tip>
