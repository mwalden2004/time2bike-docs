A **practice** is a session your team runs using Time2Bike's timing tools,
without public registration or checkout. Team staff access it from the team
workspace.

Open the **Practices** tab. You'll see it if you're a **coach**, **manager**,
team **admin**, or **owner**.

<Note>
  Active roster athletes are added when you create a practice. Re-sync the
  roster if athletes join later, and add drop-ins at the timer when needed.
</Note>

## Creating a practice

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click New practice">
    <Frame caption="A new practice with a time trial and an endurance activity. Demo data.">
      <img src="/images/teams/new-practice.png" alt="A new practice with a time trial and an endurance activity." />
    </Frame>
  </Step>
  <Step title="Name it and set the date and time">
    `Tuesday intervals`. Location is optional.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add activities">
    A practice holds one or more **activities**, and each activity is timed
    separately. Pick from:

    | Activity | What it runs |
    |---|---|
    | **Time trial** | Per-rider gate starts, several riders on course at once |
    | **Endurance / lap ride** | Open-ended lap accumulation, credits each lap and tracks best lap |
    | **Dual slalom** | Head-to-head brackets |
    | **Group ride / mass start** | Everyone off at once |
    | **Short track** | Lap-based short track |

    Endurance and short track let you set a target lap count. Leave it blank
    for an open-ended ride.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create it">
    The practice appears on the calendar.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Two activities at once

This is the point of activities. Each one gets **its own clock**, so a time
trial, an endurance lap ride, and a dual-slalom bracket can run at the same time
under one practice, each on a different phone with a different coach. Coordinate
staff, riders, and course use even when the timing clocks are separate.

The operator screen picks the right surface per activity automatically.

## Timing a practice

Every practice row has a **Time it** button that drops you straight onto the
operator screen. Today's practice (or the next one, or the most recent) is
pulled out into a loud card at the top of the page, because on a phone at the
trailhead that should be the only thing you need to hit.

From there, everything in the [Live Timing](/timing/overview) documentation
applies: recording finishes, fixing mistakes, offline capture, and so on.

<Tip>
  Drop-ins who aren't on the roster can be added at the timer. You don't have to
  fix the roster first.
</Tip>

## Calendar and list views

- **Calendar** — the month, with practice days highlighted. Tap a day to see
  what's on it. Opens on the month that actually has something in it.
- **List** — grouped by month, newest first.

## Seasons

Create a season (`Fall 2026`) and tag practices to it. Then filter the practice
list, and the [clipboard](/teams/clipboard), to one season at a time.

Seasons are created from the **Results** tab, under **New season**.

## Re-syncing the roster

The refresh icon on a practice row re-enters your current roster. Use it when
athletes joined the team after you created the practice. They won't be in it
otherwise.

## Who can access practice records

Coaches, managers, team admins, and owners can use the practice timing and
results tools. Ordinary roster athletes do not gain that staff access.
Practices are excluded from public event listings. Review staff roles and take
care when sharing screenshots or exported results.
