Time2Bike handles registration payments through **Stripe**. You connect a Stripe
account for your organization. Registration charges are made on that connected
account, and Stripe manages settlement and payouts. Check Stripe for your
account's payout schedule, verification requirements, holds, and reserves.

## Connecting

Once per organization: **Settings → Billing → Connect Stripe**.

Stripe asks for your business details, bank account, and identity verification.
Onboarding and verification times vary. Check that Stripe has enabled payments
and that Time2Bike shows the account as connected before opening paid registration.

→ [Stripe payouts](/organizers/organization/settings-billing)

<Note>
  Free events don't need any of this. Connect Stripe when you start charging.
</Note>

## What a rider pays

| Line | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Entry fee (and any merch or add-ons) | You |
| **Service fee** — the amount shown before purchase | Time2Bike |

The service fee is added on top and shown separately on the rider's receipt. It
doesn't come out of your entry fee.

Stripe charges its own card processing fee, which Stripe deducts before paying
you out. That's between you and Stripe, at whatever rate your account is on.

## Getting paid

Stripe displays your payout schedule and expected arrival dates. Bank processing,
account reviews, reserves, or other holds can affect when funds arrive.

**Settings → Billing → Open Stripe dashboard** takes you to your own Stripe
dashboard for payout history, statements, tax forms, and bank details.

<Warning>
  Missing verification information can delay payouts. Check your Stripe dashboard
  for outstanding requirements and the status of the payout.
</Warning>

## Money taken at the desk

The [check-in desk](/organizers/events/check-in) can take payment on race
morning, two ways: a QR code the rider pays on their own phone, or a card field
on your tablet for when the desk has signal and the rider doesn't.

Both use your connected Stripe account. Review the amount and service fee shown
before collecting payment, then confirm the payment status at the desk. Stripe's
settlement and refund processing still apply.

Entry fees and one-day licence fees are charged separately, so a rider who owes
only a licence isn't asked for an entry as well.

Cash and waived entries take no card and move no money — they only mark the
entry paid, with a note of which.

<Note>
  Card payments at the desk need a connected Stripe account, the same as online
  registration. Cash and waived work without one.
</Note>

## Refunds

Submit refunds from **Pricing → Refunds** to the original payment method.
Submission does not confirm that funds have reached the rider's bank.

- Review the remaining refundable amount before submitting. Registration refunds
  cover the entry and one-day license fee, less discounts and earlier refunds;
  other purchases may need their own refund action.
- Ordinary organizer refunds exclude the platform service fee. The
  [Terms of Service](https://time2.bike/legal/terms#refunds) describe the full-charge
  refund exceptions for confirmed fraud and duplicate or failed charges caused
  by a platform fault. Contact support about those cases.
- Stripe's original processing fees are not returned. Insufficient available
  balance or bank processing can delay or prevent a refund. Check the payment
  in Stripe. See [Stripe's refund guidance](https://docs.stripe.com/refunds).

→ [Refunds](/organizers/pricing/refunds)

## Chargebacks

Monitor your Stripe dashboard for payment disputes and response deadlines.
Submit relevant records there, such as the registration confirmation, applicable
refund policy, and evidence of participation. Providing records does not
guarantee that a dispute will be decided in your favor.
