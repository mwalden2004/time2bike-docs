Time2Bike handles registration payments through **Stripe**. You connect a Stripe
account once, riders pay through it, and the money lands in your bank on Stripe's
normal schedule.

Time2Bike never holds your money.

## Connecting

Once per organization: **Settings → Billing → Connect Stripe**.

Stripe asks for your business details, bank account, and identity verification.
Budget a day or two. It isn't instant, and you can't publish a paid event until
it's done.

→ [Stripe payouts](/organizers/organization/settings-billing)

<Note>
  Free events don't need any of this. Connect Stripe when you start charging.
</Note>

## What a rider pays

| Line | Goes to |
|---|---|
| Entry fee (and any merch or add-ons) | You |
| **Service fee** — 3% of their subtotal | Time2Bike |

The service fee is added on top and shown separately on the rider's receipt. It
doesn't come out of your entry fee.

Stripe charges its own card processing fee, which Stripe deducts before paying
you out. That's between you and Stripe, at whatever rate your account is on.

## Getting paid

Money lands in your bank on Stripe's payout schedule, typically daily once
your account is fully verified.

**Settings → Billing → Open Stripe dashboard** takes you to your own Stripe
dashboard for payout history, statements, tax forms, and bank details.

<Warning>
  If your Stripe account isn't fully verified, Stripe holds payouts until it is.
  If money hasn't arrived, that's the first thing to check.
</Warning>

## Refunds

Issue refunds from Time2Bike under **Pricing → Refunds**, and they go back to
the rider's original card.

- Your Stripe balance decreases by the refunded amount.
- The service fee is **not** returned, on a full refund or a partial one. It is earned when the entry is taken.
- The one exception is a charge that should never have existed — a fraudulent charge, or a duplicate caused by a platform fault — where the whole charge including the service fee comes back.

→ [Refunds](/organizers/pricing/refunds)

## Chargebacks

If a rider disputes a charge with their bank, Stripe handles it and Time2Bike
emails you. Respond in your Stripe dashboard with whatever evidence you have:
the signed waiver, the registration confirmation, the start list.
