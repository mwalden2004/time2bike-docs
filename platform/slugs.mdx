---
title: "Slugs"
description: "The readable web addresses your organization, events, and series live at."
---

A **slug** is the readable part of a web address — the `mountain-bike-club` in `time2.bike/mountain-bike-club`. Organizations, events, and series each get one.

## The rules

- Lowercase letters, numbers, and hyphens only.
- Can't start or end with a hyphen.
- Organization addresses are unique across all of Time2Bike; event addresses are
  unique within your organization.

If what you want is taken, the picker tells you and suggests something close.

## Where each one goes

| URL | Resolves to |
|---|---|
| `time2.bike/[slug]` | Organization public page |
| `time2.bike/[slug]/store` | Organization merch storefront |
| `time2.bike/events/[event-slug]` | Event public page |
| `time2.bike/events/[event-slug]/register` | Event registration flow |
| `time2.bike/events/[event-slug]/results` | Event results |
| `time2.bike/events/[event-slug]/live` | Event live results |
| `time2.bike/series/[series-slug]` | Series public page |
| `time2.bike/profiles/[participantId]` | Rider public profile (note: uses the participant ID, not a slug) |

## Changing one

You can change an organization, event, or series address from its settings page
at any time.

<Warning>
  **Old addresses don't redirect.** Every link anyone has already shared — on a
  flyer, in an email, on Facebook — stops working the moment you change it.

  If you have to change one, do it before you publicise anything, and search
  your own emails and posts for the old link afterwards.
</Warning>

## Words you can't use

A handful of words are part of Time2Bike's own addresses and can't be taken — `auth`, `dashboard`, `account`, `organizations`, `events`, `series`, `profiles`, `timing`, and a few others. The picker rejects them and offers an alternative.
