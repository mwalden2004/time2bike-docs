---
title: "Notifications"
description: "Which emails Time2Bike sends, when."
---

Time2Bike sends a small, predictable set of transactional emails. There is no marketing email infrastructure — bring your own ESP for that.

## To riders

| When | Subject |
|---|---|
| Registration confirmed | "You're registered for [Event]" — receipt + race details. |
| Registration updated | "Your [Event] registration was updated". |
| Refund issued | "Refund processed for [Event]". |
| Merch order confirmed | "Order #N — [Org]". |
| Merch order fulfilled | "Your order is ready / shipped". |
| Results published | "Your [Event] results are live". |
| Rider invite | "Join Time2Bike to complete your registration" (when an organizer manually adds a rider). |
| Sign-in code (MFA) | "Your Time2Bike sign-in code is [6-digit]". |
| Account email change | Verification link to the new address. |

## To organizers

| When | Subject |
|---|---|
| Team invite | "You've been invited to [Org]". |
| Stripe action required | "Update your payout details for [Org]" (mirrors Stripe's notice). |
| Daily digest | Optional: registrations + revenue summary (opt-in per user). |

## Sender

All email is sent from `no-reply@time2.bike` (display name: `Time2Bike`). For rider-facing emails the footer includes your org name and a link to your public page.

## Opting out

Riders can disable all non-essential email under **Account → Privacy**. Receipts and confirmations are not opt-out-able — they're transactional and required.

## Templates

Email templates are currently fixed. Custom branding and per-org template editing is on the roadmap. Today, your org name, logo, and primary color are inserted automatically.
