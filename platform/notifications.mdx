Time2Bike sends account messages and supports organizer communications about
entries and events. Which messages are sent depends on the action and the
organizer's settings. Email delivery can be delayed, blocked, or filtered as spam.

## Account and event messages

Messages can include account verification, sign-in codes, password resets,
registration confirmations, rider invitations, and organizer updates. Exact
subjects and contents vary. Check your account or event dashboard for the
current registration, payment, or result status.

A missing email does not establish that a payment failed or an entry was lost.
Check the relevant record before paying or registering again. Organizers should
also monitor Stripe directly for payout requirements and dispute deadlines.

## Sender and replies

Time2Bike sends through its email service. Organizer messages can use the
organization's name, branding, and reply-to address. Check the reply-to address
before replying; support and event questions may need different recipients.

<Tip>
  Keep your organization's contact and reply-to details current. Include an
  event contact address in the description so riders can reach the organizer
  if an email does not arrive.
</Tip>

## Communication preferences

Riders can withdraw consent for organizer text messages under **Account →
Privacy**, or reply **STOP**. Sign-in and verification messages are separate
from organizer message consent. See [Privacy](/riders/account/privacy).

There is no general email opt-out control in account settings. For unwanted
organizer messages, use an unsubscribe option if the message provides one,
or contact the sender. Contact Time2Bike support if you need help. Read the
[Privacy Policy](https://time2.bike/legal/privacy#communications) for communication
practices and the distinction between account messages and promotional email.

## Organizer messages

Use rider contact details for the purposes described in the
[organizer terms](https://time2.bike/legal/terms#organizers). Access to a contact
list does not establish consent to send promotional messages. Review the
intended recipients and message before sending.

Configured branding can be included in organizer messages. Check the preview
for the correct name, logo, reply-to address, and links.
