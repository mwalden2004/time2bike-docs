Every image on Time2Bike is uploaded the same way, whether it's an org logo, an
event banner, a gallery photo, a product shot or a sponsor logo: click the
field, pick a file, done.

There's no media library to keep tidy. Images attach to whatever you were
editing.

## What to upload

| Where | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization logo** | Square | Shown in the nav, on your public page, and in emails. Rendered as a circle in some places, so keep the important part centred |
| **Event banner** | Wide, 16:9 | The hero image on your event page, and the preview when someone shares the link |
| **Team logo** | Square | 500 × 500 is plenty |
| **Team banner** | Wide, around 1600 × 400 | |
| **Sponsor logo** | Any | PNG with a transparent background looks best |
| **Product photo** | Square | |
| **Gallery photos** | Any | |

**Upload limit:** 10 MB. Use PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF for images. Export SVG
logos to PNG before uploading.

Use photos and logos you have permission to publish. Public-page images can be
viewed and copied by others. Keep private documents, contact details, and
identifying file names out of these image uploads.

## Sizing and formats are handled for you

Pages that use the image optimizer request sizes suited to their layout and
can use AVIF or WebP where supported. Large originals can still affect upload
and loading time. Preview your images on a phone after publishing.

## Alt text

Where an image field provides an alt-text option, add a short description for
people using screen readers. Include essential event information in the page
text as well.

One plain sentence: "Riders climbing the fire road at sunrise." Not "banner" or
"image1".

## Replacing an image

Upload a replacement and save the change. The page will use the new image,
but previously loaded pages and cached previews may still show the old one.

<Note>
  If you still see the old banner, refresh and check that your change saved.
  Browsers and social platforms can keep cached copies for a while.
</Note>

## Deleting

Removing an image from an event or product removes its reference from that
page. It does not confirm deletion of the uploaded file or copies elsewhere.
For a file-removal request, contact support with the image and page links.
Copies already downloaded or cached by others may remain.

## Making banners look good

- **Shoot wide.** Banners are cropped tighter on phones, so keep the subject
  near the middle.
- **Avoid text baked into the image.** It'll be unreadable on a phone, and screen
  readers can't reach it.
- **Bright and high-contrast wins.** Your banner is the preview image when
  somebody shares your event on social, so it's competing in a busy feed.
