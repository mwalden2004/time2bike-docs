Every image on Time2Bike — org logos, event banners, gallery photos, product
shots, sponsor logos — is uploaded the same way: click the field, pick a file,
done.

There's no media library to keep tidy. Images attach to whatever you were
editing.

## What to upload

| Where | Shape | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| **Organization logo** | Square | Shown in the nav, on your public page, and in emails. Rendered as a circle in some places, so keep the important part centred |
| **Event banner** | Wide, 16:9 | The hero image on your event page, and the preview when someone shares the link |
| **Team logo** | Square | 500 × 500 is plenty |
| **Team banner** | Wide, around 1600 × 400 | |
| **Sponsor logo** | Any | PNG with a transparent background looks best |
| **Product photo** | Square | |
| **Gallery photos** | Any | |

**Limits:** 5 MB per image. PNG, JPEG, WebP, or GIF — plus SVG for logos.

## Alt text

Every image field asks for a short description. It's read aloud to riders using
a screen reader, and search engines use it too.

One plain sentence: "Riders climbing the fire road at sunrise." Not "banner" or
"image1".

## Replacing an image

Upload a new one over the top. The old one stops being used immediately.

<Note>
  If you replace a banner and still see the old one, refresh the page — your
  browser is showing you its cached copy. Riders loading the page fresh get the
  new one.
</Note>

## Deleting

Removing an image from an event or product takes it off that page. If you need a
file taken down entirely — you uploaded the wrong photo, or someone asked to be
removed — contact support and we'll purge it.

## Making banners look good

- **Shoot wide.** Banners are cropped tighter on phones, so keep the subject
  near the middle.
- **Avoid text baked into the image.** It'll be unreadable on a phone, and screen
  readers can't reach it.
- **Bright and high-contrast wins.** Your banner is the preview image when
  somebody shares your event on social — it's competing in a busy feed.
